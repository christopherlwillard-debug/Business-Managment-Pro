Current Problem

The accounting engine is automatically converting my calculated Take-Home into an Owner Withdrawal / Owner Draw.

That is incorrect.

My Take-Home is compensation earned by working the completed job.

It is not an equity withdrawal.

Because of this, the same money is being represented multiple times throughout the Accounting & Tax section.

Currently I have:

* Owner Capital
* Take-Home Dashboard
* My Pay
* Owner Withdrawals

These screens overlap and create duplicate accounting.

⸻

The Job Financials Should Remain Exactly As They Are

No changes are needed to the Job Financials calculation.

It already correctly calculates:

Revenue

↓

Expenses

↓

5% Business Growth

↓

Hourly Labor

↓

50/50 Profit Split

↓

My Share

↓

Tax Set-Aside

↓

My Take-Home

This workflow should remain unchanged.

⸻

What Needs To Change

The accounting engine needs to change what happens after the job is finalized.

Instead of creating:

Owner Draw

it should create:

Owner Compensation

or

Owner Job Pay

Those are two completely different accounting transactions.

⸻

Owner Capital

Owner Capital should ONLY track money that I personally invest into the business.

Examples:

• Startup money

• Personal money used to buy equipment

• Personal cash deposited into the business

Job payments should never change Owner Capital.

Owner Capital should only increase or decrease from actual capital contributions or capital returns.

⸻

My Pay

The My Pay screen should become the primary source of truth for owner compensation.

It already tracks:

Earned

Paid

Remaining Owed

That is exactly what it should do.

However, these transactions should no longer be labeled as Owner Withdrawals.

They should be labeled:

Owner Compensation

Owner Pay

Owner Job Pay

or

Job Compensation

because they were earned through completed jobs.

⸻

Take-Home Dashboard

The Take-Home Dashboard should not be a completely separate accounting calculation.

Instead, it should simply summarize the My Pay ledger by month.

For example:

January

$1,240

February

$2,110

March

$819

April

$1,531

May

$1,905

June

$2,703

This dashboard should read from the exact same Owner Compensation data already stored in My Pay.

There should only be one source of truth.

⸻

Owner Withdrawals

Owner Withdrawals should become a manual accounting transaction.

They should only be used when I intentionally remove business equity that was NOT earned from a completed job.

Example:

Business has accumulated cash.

I decide to take an extra $5,000 from the business that was not tied to any completed job.

That is an Owner Withdrawal.

This should be rare.

Completed jobs should never automatically generate Owner Withdrawal transactions.

⸻

Record Withdrawal Button

The current “Record Withdrawal” button is misleading because it is used for owner compensation.

Rename this button to something such as:

Record Owner Pay

Pay Myself

Record Payment

Transfer to Personal

Only use the word “Withdrawal” when recording a true equity withdrawal.

⸻

Desired Accounting Flow

Job Finalized

↓

Job Financials calculates My Take-Home

↓

Accounting creates an Owner Compensation record

↓

Owner Compensation updates:

• My Pay

• Monthly Take-Home Dashboard

↓

Owner Capital remains unchanged

↓

Owner Withdrawals remain at $0 unless I manually record a true equity withdrawal.

⸻

Expected Result

After this change:

* Job Financials will continue calculating compensation exactly as they do today.
* My Take-Home will no longer be posted as an Owner Draw.
* My Pay and Take-Home Dashboard will use the same underlying Owner Compensation data.
* Owner Capital will only reflect personal investments into the business.
* Owner Withdrawals will only represent true equity withdrawals and will no longer be created automatically from completed jobs.

This preserves the existing Job Financials system while correcting the accounting logic so that owner compensation, owner equity, and owner withdrawals are treated as three separate accounting concepts.
