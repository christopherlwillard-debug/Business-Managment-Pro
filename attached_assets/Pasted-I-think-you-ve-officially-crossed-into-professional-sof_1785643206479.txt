I'd only add one thing.
Don't calculate guides during auto-scroll.

Eventually you'll allow dragging near the edge of the canvas.

When that happens...

Drag

↓

Canvas auto-scrolls

↓

Guide calculations temporarily pause

↓

Resume after scrolling stabilizes

Otherwise you'll get jitter.

I'd literally add

Auto-scroll handling

If the canvas auto-scrolls while dragging, temporarily suspend guide snapping until the scroll delta settles.

Guide overlays should reposition after the scroll completes.

Prevent jitter caused by simultaneous scrolling and snapping.
I'd also add
Guide priority

When multiple guides are possible simultaneously:

Center alignment has higher priority than edge alignment.

Edge alignment has higher priority than spacing indicators.

Spacing indicators never override an active snap.

Otherwise weird snapping happens.
