What I would tell Replit

I'd actually stop trying to patch #817 and replace it with:

Replace Service Card Inspector Integration

The current approach of injecting CardItemEditorPanel into the existing section inspector is creating state conflicts and poor UX.

Instead of attempting further specialContent overrides, implement a dedicated Card Editor mode.

Requirements:

Clicking a card opens a dedicated Card Editor panel.
Card Editor is independent of Quick / Content / Style / Layout / Advanced section tabs.
Card Editor contains:
Content
Icon/Image
Design
Layout
Presets
Panel header shows:
Card title
Back button ("Back to Services")
Clicking Back returns to the Services section inspector.
Section settings remain unchanged while editing cards.
Card editing state is isolated from section editing state.
No reuse of section inspector tabs for card editing.

Goal:

Cards become first-class editable objects with their own editor experience rather than attempting to share the Services section inspector.

From what I see in your screenshot, continuing to force card editing into the existing section inspector is likely going to keep causing issues. A dedicated card editor will be simpler, cleaner, and much easier to extend when you add Team cards, Testimonial cards, Pricing cards, Certification cards, and Portfolio cards later.
