1179 - Sub Pay: Dual Rates, Advances & Performance Bonus

What & Why

Three improvements to the hourly subcontractor payment workflow, designed to make every payment self-documenting while future-proofing the ledger for reporting, audits, and 1099 preparation.

1. Advance Against Wages (replaces “Deduction”)

When Willard fronts money to a subcontractor during the day (lunch, fuel, tools, cash, etc.), it should be recorded as an Advance Against Wages, not a deduction.

The advance simply reduces the amount of cash paid at the end of the job because the subcontractor has already received part of their compensation.

Using the term Advance throughout the database and API leaves room for true payroll deductions (taxes, insurance, garnishments, retirement, etc.) in the future without requiring schema changes or field renaming.

2. Separate Work Rate and Travel Rate

Hourly subcontractors are often paid different rates depending on what they were doing.

Example:

* Work Time: $25.00/hour
* Travel Time: $15.00/hour

The payment dialog should support independent hour/rate pairs so both the calculation and payment history accurately reflect how the subcontractor was paid.

3. Performance Bonus

Allow an optional one-time dollar bonus for exceptional work.

A bonus includes an optional note explaining why it was given and is added before any advance is subtracted.

⸻

Done Looks Like

The payment summary always displays:

Work Pay + Travel Pay + Bonus − Advance = Net Paid

Example:

Base Pay: $420.00

Performance Bonus: +$50.00

Advance: −$15.00

Net Paid: $455.00

⸻

Every payment history entry permanently shows the complete breakdown.

Example:

Paid: $455.00

Work:
12.0 hrs × $25.00 = $300.00

Travel:
8.0 hrs × $15.00 = $120.00

Performance Bonus:
+$50.00
“Finished punch list ahead of schedule”

Advance:
−$15.00
“Lunch”

Net Paid:
$455.00

The payment ledger becomes completely self-documenting, making it easy to understand exactly how every payment was calculated months or years later.

⸻

The subcontractor profile includes a default Travel Rate alongside the existing hourly pay rate.

Every payment stores all information required to completely reconstruct the calculation:

* Work Hours
* Work Rate
* Travel Hours
* Travel Rate
* Bonus Amount
* Bonus Note
* Advance Amount
* Advance Note
* Net Paid

Base Pay is calculated from the stored hours and rates rather than stored separately, preventing duplicate data and ensuring calculations always remain consistent.

⸻

Out of Scope

* Fixed-pay subcontractors
* Allocation/pay-split subcontractors
* Payroll deductions (taxes, insurance, garnishments, retirement, etc.)
* Reporting grouped by work vs. travel hours
* Payroll processing

⸻

Steps

1. Extend sub_payment_records

Add nullable columns:

* workHours
* workRate
* travelHours
* travelRate
* bonusAmount
* bonusNote
* advanceAmount
* advanceNote
* netPaid

Run the ALTER statements using the existing startup migration pattern in:

artifacts/api-server/src/index.ts

⸻

2. Add Travel Rate

Add:

* travelPayRate to subcontractors
* travelRate to job_subcontractors

These provide a profile default with optional per-job overrides.

⸻

3. Update Pay Worker API

Accept:

* workHours
* workRate
* travelHours
* travelRate
* bonusAmount
* bonusNote
* advanceAmount
* advanceNote

Server calculates:

Work Pay = workHours × workRate

Travel Pay = travelHours × travelRate

Base Pay = Work Pay + Travel Pay

Net Paid = Base Pay + Bonus Amount − Advance Amount

Store:

* Net Paid as the payment amount
* Every payment component individually
* A readable payment description summarizing the breakdown

⸻

4. Subcontractor Profile

Add a Travel Rate field next to the existing hourly rate so defaults can be configured once per subcontractor.

⸻

5. Pay Worker Dialog

Replace the single hourly section with:

Work

* Hours
* Rate

Travel

* Hours
* Rate

Performance Bonus (optional)

* Dollar amount
* Note

Advance Against Wages (optional)

* Dollar amount
* Note

Examples:

* Lunch
* Fuel
* Tool Purchase
* Cash Advance

Summary displayed live:

Work Pay + Travel Pay + Bonus − Advance = Net Paid

Existing partial payment and pay-in-full workflows remain unchanged.

⸻

6. Payment History

Each payment entry displays:

* Work hours × rate
* Travel hours × rate
* Performance bonus with note
* Advance with note
* Net paid

Older payment records created before this feature continue displaying only the recorded payment amount for backward compatibility.
