I think this is about 90% there. There are just a couple of things I'd tweak.

1. Rename "Revenue" to "Job Revenue"

Throughout BuildPro you've consistently used "Job Revenue." I'd keep that wording so users don't wonder if it's gross company revenue or job revenue.

2. Rename "Job Expenses" to "Materials & Expenses"

Since this is the same number used later in Profit Split, the wording should stay consistent.

Instead of:

Job Expenses

Use:

Materials & Expenses

That reinforces that it's everything reducing profit, not just receipts entered as expenses.

3. Don't call it a "Profit Split header"

Call it a Financial Summary.

Something like:

Financial Summary

Job Revenue

Materials & Expenses

Net Business Profit

Estimated Fuel Cost

Estimated Operational Profit

That reads much better than saying the Profit Split screen starts with another Profit Split section.

4. Keep the Trip Documentation Packet

I wouldn't bury it somewhere random.

Move it to the very bottom of the Profit Split page under a small section:

Additional Reports

Trip Documentation Packet

That keeps it discoverable without cluttering the financial summary.

5. One more cleanup

Since you're already deleting the Job Financials card, I'd also remove the duplicate "+$2,411.43" number that was only acting as a button label.

The only place Net Business Profit should appear on the Job Card is inside the P&L widget.

I'd add this at the end
Design Principle

Every major number should appear only once on the Job Card.

The Job Card provides the summary.
The Profit Split screen provides the explanation.
The Expense Drill-down provides the supporting detail.

Users should never need to compare two sections showing the same numbers to understand the job's financial status.

I think this is one of the highest-value cleanup tasks you've written. It doesn't remove any capability, doesn't change any calculations, and doesn't force users into more screens. It simply removes UI that isn't earning its space while keeping your philosophy intact of seeing almost everything about the job from one place.
