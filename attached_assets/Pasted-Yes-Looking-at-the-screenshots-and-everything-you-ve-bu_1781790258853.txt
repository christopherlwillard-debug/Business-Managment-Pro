Now I have the full picture across all four problem areas. Here's my honest assessment before we create any tasks:

1. Bank Reconcile vs. Banking Page
Your instinct is right — the 6 AM daily Plaid sync automatically creates all those "Chase – BUS COMPLETE CHK" batches you see in the Reconcile tab. The Banking page already reads the same transaction data. You are paying for Plaid API calls that feed both places. The Reconcile tab was originally designed for manually uploading CSV bank statements to verify against records you'd already entered. Now that the Banking page handles Plaid live, the Reconcile tab is mostly duplicated noise — and the daily auto-batching is the cost center.

2. Settings duplication
You have two layers: a "Main Company Defaults" (LLC name, EIN, legal entity, global address) and a "per-business profile" (DBA name, phone, email, address, logo) — both in the Business tab, stacked on top of each other with overlapping fields. Now that you have proper per-business views, this double-entry is confusing and dated. The fix is clearer separation, not data deletion.

3. Asset business-use % on Year-End report
The Year-End report shows the % read-only. To change it you have to leave, go to Assets, expand the row, and edit it there. The backend API for updating it already exists (PUT /api/assets/:id/business-use) — it just needs an edit button on the Year-End page.

4. Job card sub-payment label
In your "Paid" section (where the client has paid the invoice), cards with outstanding worker/sub pay show a big bold "UNPAID JOB PAYMENTS" header — which looks like the job itself hasn't been paid. It's the opposite of what that section means. It should say "WORKER PAYMENTS DUE" or similar so it's clearly about paying your subs, not about the client payment.
