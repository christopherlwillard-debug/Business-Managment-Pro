1341 - Redesign Accounting Overview — 3 Clean Sections

What & Why

The Accounting & Tax Overview currently repeats the same financial information across multiple cards (Business Reserve, Overhead Coverage, Tax & Growth Reserve, Net Profit hero, and summary metrics). The goal is to simplify the page into three clear sections, each answering one specific question while reducing visual clutter by approximately 40–50%.

Each section should have a single responsibility:

* Business Reserve Dashboard → “How much cash should stay in my business checking account?”
* Take-Home Pay → “How much money have I actually taken home?”
* Business Performance → “How is the business performing overall?”

All existing calculations remain unchanged. This is a UI/UX redesign only.
