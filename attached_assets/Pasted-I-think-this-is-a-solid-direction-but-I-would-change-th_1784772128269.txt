I think this is a solid direction, but I would change the data model now before you build it. Otherwise you'll paint yourself into a corner when someone changes their tax election.

I would change the settings to this
Business Tax Profile

Legal Entity

Sole Proprietorship
Single-Member LLC
Multi-Member LLC
Corporation

Federal Tax Election

Sole Proprietorship
Partnership
S Corporation
C Corporation

Effective Date

Date the tax election became effective

For a new business:

Legal Entity = Single-Member LLC
Federal Tax Election = Sole Proprietorship
Effective Date = Business start date (or today's date)

When you elect S corp later, you would simply update:

Legal Entity = Single-Member LLC (unchanged)
Federal Tax Election = S Corporation
Effective Date = 2027-01-01 (for example)

Then reports automatically use the correct rules for each tax year.

I would not derive Tax Treatment from Entity Type

This is the only part of your proposal I disagree with.

Right now you have:

Tax Treatment: auto-derived from entity type (read-only)

That isn't flexible enough.

A Single-Member LLC can legitimately be taxed as:

Sole Proprietorship
S Corporation
C Corporation

A Multi-Member LLC can legitimately be taxed as:

Partnership
S Corporation
C Corporation

So "Tax Treatment" should be configurable within the valid choices for that entity—not permanently derived.

I would also rename the accounting logic

Instead of:

computeNetProfit reads the tax profile

I'd say:

computeNetProfit reads the Federal Tax Election.

That's what actually determines how owner draws, distributions, and payroll are treated.

Add a validation rule

Prevent invalid combinations.

For example:

Legal Entity	Allowed Federal Tax Elections
Sole Proprietorship	Sole Proprietorship
Single-Member LLC	Sole Proprietorship, S Corporation, C Corporation
Multi-Member LLC	Partnership, S Corporation, C Corporation
Corporation	C Corporation, S Corporation

That keeps the UI simple while ensuring users can't select combinations that don't make sense.

One more thing I'd add

I would make the "Owner Activity" card completely data-driven.

Instead of hardcoding labels like:

Owner Draws
Member Draws
Shareholder Distributions

Have the tax profile provide:

Activity title
Contribution label
Distribution/Draw label
Disclaimer

Then the frontend simply renders whatever the profile supplies. That keeps business rules centralized and makes future changes much easier.

Overall

I'd rate this 9.8/10.

The only significant change I'd make is:

Separate Legal Entity from Federal Tax Election.
Add an Effective Date so historical reports remain accurate after a tax election change.
Validate allowed combinations instead of making tax treatment permanently read-only.

Those additions will give your accounting engine a foundation that can support both your current single-member LLC and a future S corporation without requiring another architectural redesign.
