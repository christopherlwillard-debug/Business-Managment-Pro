1410 - Fix Owner Compensation vs. Owner Withdrawal Accounting

What & Why

The Job Financials engine already calculates my compensation correctly. Every completed job follows my established workflow:

Revenue
→ Expenses
→ 5% Business Growth
→ Hourly Employee/Subcontractor Pay
→ 50/50 Profit Split
→ My Share
→ Tax Set-Aside
→ My Take-Home

This calculation is correct and should not be changed.

The problem occurs after the job is finalized. The accounting engine is currently posting my earned Take-Home as an "Owner Draw" expense. This incorrectly treats compensation earned from working a completed job as an equity withdrawal and causes the same money to appear in multiple places throughout the application (Owner Capital, Take-Home Dashboard, My Pay, and Owner Withdrawals).

The accounting system must distinguish between two completely different concepts:

• Owner Job Pay (Owner Compensation) – Money earned by completing jobs and working in the business.
• Owner Withdrawal – A manual equity withdrawal that is not connected to any completed job.

These should never be treated as the same accounting transaction.

Business Rules

The Job Financials calculation must remain exactly as it works today.

No changes should be made to:

• Revenue
• Expenses
• 5% Business Growth
• Hourly Employee/Subcontractor Pay
• Profit Split
• Tax Reserve
• My Take-Home calculation

Only the accounting transaction created after the job is finalized should change.

New Accounting Behavior

When a completed job calculates My Take-Home, the accounting engine should create an Owner Compensation (Owner Job Pay) transaction instead of an Owner Draw.

Owner Compensation represents compensation earned from working completed jobs.

Owner Withdrawal represents a manual equity withdrawal unrelated to job compensation.

These are separate accounting events.

Accounting Integrity Rules

Owner Compensation must NEVER:

• Reduce Gross Profit a second time.
• Reduce Net Profit a second time.
• Reduce Business Equity.
• Reduce Owner Capital.
• Be treated as an operating expense on the Profit & Loss statement.

Owner Compensation exists only to track:

• Earned
• Paid
• Remaining Owed

It is simply a payment-tracking transaction for owner compensation.

Owner Capital Rules

Owner Capital must remain completely independent from owner compensation.

Owner Capital should only read from owner_capital_entries.

Owner Capital should only change from:

• Capital Contributions
• Capital Returns

Owner Capital must never be affected by:

• Owner Compensation
• Owner Withdrawals
• Revenue
• Job Profit
• Take-Home
• My Pay calculations

Owner Compensation Rules

Every completed job should automatically create an Owner Compensation record.

Each record should contain:

• Job Number
• Job Name
• Completion Date
• Earned Amount
• Amount Paid
• Remaining Balance
• Payment Date
• Notes

The Owner Compensation ledger becomes the single source of truth for owner pay.

Payment Status

Each Owner Compensation entry should support:

• Earned
• Partially Paid
• Paid

Example

Earned:
$1,125

Paid:
$700

Remaining Owed:
$425

The unpaid balance remains money owed to the owner.

It is not an Owner Withdrawal.

Owner Withdrawals

Owner Withdrawals become a completely separate manual accounting feature.

Purpose:

Manual removal of business equity that is NOT connected to a completed job.

Example:

The business has accumulated excess cash and I intentionally transfer $5,000 to myself outside of the normal job compensation process.

That is an Owner Withdrawal.

Completed jobs should NEVER automatically create Owner Withdrawal transactions.

UI Changes

Rename all "Record Owner Draw" buttons to:

Record Owner Pay

Rename the Dashboard "Record Withdrawal" button to:

Record Owner Pay

Add a separate secondary button or link:

Record Equity Withdrawal

This action should clearly state that it is for manual equity withdrawals only and is expected to be used rarely.

Rename UI Labels

Current:

Recent Withdrawals

New:

Recent Owner Pay

or

Recent Owner Payments

The wording should accurately reflect that these are payments earned from completed jobs, not equity withdrawals.

Owner Pay Summary API

Update the owner-pay-summary endpoint to include both:

Owner Compensation
Owner Draw

This maintains backward compatibility with historical data while allowing all new records to use the new category.

Filter:

category IN ('Owner Compensation', 'Owner Draw')

No data migration is required.

Take-Home Dashboard

Rewrite the Take-Home Dashboard so it no longer performs an independent calculation.

Instead, it should summarize the Owner Compensation ledger by month.

Example:

January
$1,240

February
$2,105

March
$819

April
$1,531

May
$1,905

June
$2,703

The My Pay ledger becomes the single source of truth.

The Take-Home Dashboard simply groups the same data by month.

This removes duplicate calculations and ensures both screens always match.

Done Looks Like

✓ Job Financials remain unchanged.

✓ Completed jobs create Owner Compensation instead of Owner Draw.

✓ Owner Compensation tracks only Earned, Paid, and Remaining Owed.

✓ Owner Compensation never reduces profit or owner capital a second time.

✓ Owner Capital continues reading only from owner_capital_entries.

✓ My Pay becomes the single source of truth for owner compensation.

✓ The Take-Home Dashboard summarizes the My Pay ledger by month instead of performing a separate calculation.

✓ Record Owner Draw buttons become Record Owner Pay.

✓ Record Withdrawal becomes Record Owner Pay.

✓ Record Equity Withdrawal is available as a separate manual action.

✓ Recent Withdrawals becomes Recent Owner Pay.

✓ Existing historical Owner Draw records continue working through backward-compatible queries.

✓ Future Owner Compensation and Owner Withdrawals are tracked as two completely separate accounting concepts.

Out of Scope

• Changing the Job Financials waterfall.
• Changing Revenue, Expenses, Growth Reserve, Profit Split, or Tax calculations.
• Migrating historical Owner Draw records.
• Changing reserve-summary or tax reserve calculations.
