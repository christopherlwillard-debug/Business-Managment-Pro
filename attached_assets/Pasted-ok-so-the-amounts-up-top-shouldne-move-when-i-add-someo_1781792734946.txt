Yes. Looking at your screenshot, I think you've found a real workflow problem.

Right now the top section is acting like a live cash flow calculator, while the bottom section is acting like a subcontractor ledger.

Those are two different things.

What's happening now

James is allocated:

50% of split pool
= $855

Then you paid him:

$417.50

Now the screen is mixing:

What James should earn
What James has been paid
What James is still owed

all together.

That gets confusing fast.

What I think it should do

The top numbers should NEVER change because of payments.

The top should answer:

"How is this job's profit being allocated?"

Example:

Job Revenue           $1,800
Growth Reserve        -$90

Split Pool            $1,710

James Allocation      $855
Owner Allocation      $855

Tax Reserve           -$213.75

Owner Take Home       $641.25

Those numbers should be frozen unless:

revenue changes
split percentage changes
reserve percentage changes

Paying James should not affect any of this.

The bottom section should answer

"What do I owe people?"

For James:

Allocated: $855.00

Paid: $417.50

Balance Due: $437.50

Status: Partial

or if you override:

Allocated: $855.00

Agreed Owed: $617.50

Paid: $417.50

Balance Due: $200.00

Status: Partial
Even better

I would separate them visually.

Profit Allocation
Split Pool: $1,710

James Allocation: $855
Owner Allocation: $855

Owner Take Home: $641.25

This never changes from payments.

Subcontractor Payments
James Willard

Allocation: $855.00
Agreed Owed: $617.50

Paid: $417.50
Balance Due: $200.00

68% Complete
Another thing I notice

This line:

Your Share (50%) $855

is misleading.

Because after James agreed to only take $617.50, your actual profit increased.

The system is currently showing:

James = $855
You = $855

while also showing:

James Owed = $617.50

Those can't both be true.

Once an override exists, the allocation screen should probably show:

James Agreed Amount     $617.50

Owner Share            $1,092.50

or clearly distinguish:

Original Allocation    $855.00
Agreed Owed            $617.50

because right now the math becomes hard to follow.

If I were redesigning this panel, I'd make it follow three questions:

1. What profit exists?
Revenue
Expenses
Split Pool
2. How is profit allocated?
Owner Allocation
Sub Allocations
Reserves
3. Who still needs paid?
Subcontractor Ledger

Paid
Balance Due
Status

Mixing allocation and payment status in the same numbers is exactly what causes the confusion you're feeling. The allocation should stay fixed; the payment ledger should track what remains owed.
