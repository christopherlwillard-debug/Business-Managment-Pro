Home Dashboard V2 — Business Command Center Redesign

Objective

Completely redesign the Home Dashboard into a modern, App Store quality Business Command Center.

This is not a minimalist dashboard. The goal is to create a premium, information-rich experience that allows the owner to scroll through the health of the entire business while maintaining excellent visual hierarchy.

The dashboard should feel like a hybrid of:

* Apple Human Interface Guidelines
* Stripe Dashboard
* Linear
* Garmin Aviation Displays
* Ford Digital Instrument Cluster

NOT QuickBooks.
NOT Apple Wallet.
NOT Bootstrap cards stacked vertically.

The experience should feel like operating a business, not reading reports.

⸻

Design Principles

The dashboard must answer four questions immediately.

What should I do first?

What job am I currently working on?

What requires my attention?

How is my business performing?

Everything on the Home screen should support one of those questions.

⸻

Visual Design System

Build a reusable design system before rebuilding screens.

Every component should use the exact same spacing and styling.

Cards

* 20px border radius
* Soft shadow
* White background
* 24px padding
* Consistent spacing
* Consistent typography
* Consistent icon size
* Large touch targets

No colored headers.

Instead use small accent colors only for icons, badges and important values.

⸻

Long-Term Product Direction

Willard is no longer just a contractor management application.

It is a Construction Business Operating System.

The Home screen should function as a Business Command Center that allows the owner to immediately:

• Capture receipts with AI

• Resume work on active jobs

• See today’s schedule

• Identify business issues

• Monitor financial health

• Review cash flow

• Manage owner pay

• View analytics

• Review recent activity

without duplicating functionality that belongs inside Job Cards.

⸻

Future Enhancements

Implement support for customizable dashboards.

Users should be able to:

• Collapse sections

• Expand sections

• Reorder sections with drag-and-drop

• Pin favorite sections

• Hide unused sections

• Customize quick actions

• Save multiple dashboard layouts

This allows every business owner to build a personalized Command Center while keeping a consistent overall design.

⸻

Final Vision

The finished product should feel like a premium native iPhone application that could compete visually with the best productivity apps on the App Store. It should preserve Willard’s depth and powerful features while dramatically improving organization, readability, and usability through a consistent design system and a dashboard centered on real daily workflows rather than duplicated summaries.
