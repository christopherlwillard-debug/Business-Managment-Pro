I think the Business Reserve Dashboard needs to become the primary cash planning widget and consolidate the other reserve cards.

1. Business Overhead should be Year-to-Date, not upcoming

The reserve dashboard should show Business Overhead (YTD) instead of “Recurring expenses due in the next 30 days.”

The goal of this dashboard is to answer:

“How much money from this year’s business activity should still remain in my checking account?”

So it should include:

* Estimated Taxes (YTD)
* Business Overhead (YTD)
* Operating Reserve (user configurable)
* Growth Fund (available amount)

The “next 30 days” recurring expense calculation belongs somewhere else, not in this dashboard.

⸻

2. Move Estimated Tax Liability into the Business Reserve Dashboard

The large standalone Estimated Tax Owed card is taking up a lot of space.

Instead, move those numbers into the Business Reserve Dashboard.

When I expand the Estimated Taxes section, show:

* Federal SE Tax
* Kentucky State Tax
* Owensboro Occupational Tax
* Total Estimated Tax Liability

That way everything affecting my required reserve is in one place.

⸻

3. Move Tax & Growth Reserve into the Business Reserve Dashboard

The current Tax & Growth Reserve card should also be merged into the Business Reserve Dashboard.

Inside the dashboard I still want to see:

Growth Fund

* Accumulated
* Invested
* Available
* Investment history
* Log Investment

Then, at the bottom, show the Tax Reserve information for reference.

⸻

4. Do NOT use Tax Reserve in the Required Reserve calculation

This is the important part.

The Required Reserve calculation should use the actual accounting-based Estimated Tax Liability, not the Tax Reserve percentage.

The Tax Reserve is simply a cash-flow planning tool based on paid jobs.

The accounting tax estimate is the real number.

So:

Required Reserve =

* Estimated Taxes (accounting)
* Business Overhead (YTD)
* Operating Reserve
* Growth Fund (toggle on/off)

Do NOT add the Tax Reserve percentage into this calculation.

⸻

5. Keep the Tax Reserve visible

I still want to see the Tax Reserve because it’s useful for comparing how much I’ve set aside versus what I actually owe.

Just move it to the bottom of the Business Reserve Dashboard as an informational section.

Example:

* Tax Reserve (25% of owner share)
* Estimated Tax Liability
* Difference (Over/Under Reserved)

That gives me an immediate comparison without affecting the Required Reserve calculation.

⸻

I think this will simplify the Accounting page a lot by replacing three separate cards (Estimated Taxes, Tax & Growth Reserve, and Business Reserve Dashboard) with one unified dashboard that contains everything needed for cash planning while still exposing the detailed breakdowns when expanded.
