Manufactured Home Pricing Engine & Automatic Invoice Population
Purpose
The current job card workflow works well and should not be changed. It is already being used for production tracking, labor analytics, reporting, knowledge base learning, and historical job data. The goal is not to redesign the job card, but to make the pricing and invoicing system intelligent behind the scenes.
The objective is simple:
When the user selects the home type (VOG, Hybrid, or Drywall) and checks the work being performed (Interior Trim, Plumbing, Exterior Trim, Deck, etc.), the system should automatically build the invoice using the correct pricing for the selected client.
The user should still be able to edit the invoice after it is created without affecting future pricing.

Current Workflow (No Changes)
The existing job card remains exactly as it is today.

Nothing changes for office staff or field crews.
No additional buttons.

No changes to production logging.
The job card continues serving one purpose:
Record what work is being performed.

Pricing Happens Behind the Scenes
Instead of entering prices manually every time, the system should automatically calculate pricing based on three things:
Selected Client
Home Type
Selected Services
The invoice is generated automatically when the user clicks:
Apply Pricing

Pricing Lookup Logic
Whenever pricing is needed, the application follows one simple rule:
Does this client have a custom price?

       YES
        │
Use Client Price
        │
        NO
        │
Use Master Service Price
This allows every dealer to have different pricing without duplicating the entire price list.

Layer 3 – Job Pricing Snapshot
This is the most important part of the system.
When the user clicks:
Apply Pricing

If Cavco changes their plumbing rate from $1,500 to $1,600 next month, previously completed jobs remain unchanged because the invoice is a snapshot of pricing at the time the work was performed.
Historical invoices should never change.

Automatic Pricing Algorithm
When Apply Pricing is pressed:
For every checked service

↓

Determine Billing Type

↓

Determine Client

↓

Determine Home Type

↓

Look for Client Override

↓

If Override Exists

Use Client Price

Else

Use Master Price

↓

Calculate Quantity

↓

Create Invoice Line Item

↓

Repeat Until Finished

Invoice Independence
After the invoice is generated, it should become completely independent from the pricing database.
The office can then:
Adjust prices
Add discounts
Remove items
Add materials
Add notes
Add additional charges
Change quantities
None of these edits should modify:
Master Pricing
Client Pricing
Future Jobs
Historical Analytics
Only that invoice changes.

Benefits of This Design
This architecture provides a scalable and maintainable pricing system without disrupting your existing workflow.
Benefits include:
No changes to the existing job card.
Preserves production data collection and labor analytics.
Automatically builds invoices from the services selected on the job.
Supports unlimited dealers with different pricing schedules.
Supports unlimited service types and billing methods.
Allows quantity-based pricing for services such as siding, skirting, and decking.
Stores invoices as permanent pricing snapshots so historical jobs never change.
Lets office staff make invoice adjustments after job completion without affecting the master price list or client-specific pricing.
Eliminates repetitive manual data entry while keeping the process simple for crews and office staff.
This design keeps the job card focused on production and data logging, while the pricing engine handles all billing logic in the background. It gives you automation without adding complexity to the screens your team uses every day, and it creates a foundation that can easily scale as you add more builders, clients, services, and pricing rules.
