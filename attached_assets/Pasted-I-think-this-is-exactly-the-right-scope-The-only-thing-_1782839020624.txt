I think this is the correct architectural direction.

The real problem isn't that the Production job card has too many fields. The problem is that three completely different business workflows are being forced into one job card.

The application should still have one unified Job system, but each workflow should have its own workspace that only displays the tools relevant to that type of work.

The three workspaces are:

Manufactured Home Production
Manufactured Home Service
General Contracting

The underlying database, invoices, financials, subcontractors, photos, scheduling, payments, and job records all remain shared. Only the user interface changes depending on the selected workflow.
