Quick Firebase Studio Implementation Tips
Prompt the agent: "Add a Settings screen accessible from the bottom nav or profile icon. Use sections/accordions for: Account & Security, Business Profile, Mileage Tracking, Invoicing, Expenses, Jobs, Subcontractors/1099, Reports, Notifications, App Preferences. Include toggles, text inputs for defaults like IRS mileage rate, logo upload, and secure options like 2FA."
Start simple: Prototype just 3–4 categories first (e.g., Mileage, Invoicing, Business Profile) → test on mobile preview → expand.
Use Firebase Auth/Firestore: Store user-specific settings in a users/{uid}/settings doc for easy read/write.
Make it role-aware: Subs see a minimal version (e.g., only notifications + profile).
These will make your app feel polished and customizable — contractors love control over rates, templates, and alerts without digging through menus.
Estimating & Bidding Section (Pre-Job Phase)
Quick estimate builder: Templates for common jobs (e.g., roofing, remodels, additions) with material/labor markups, auto-calculate totals, and PDF export for clients.
Bid tracking: Log sent bids, status (pending/won/lost), win rate stats, and reminders to follow up.
Material price lookup/integration: Simple database or future API pull for local supplier prices (e.g., lumber, concrete in KY area).
Why add it: Many contractors lose money on under-bidding — this helps you price accurately and turn estimates into jobs faster.
2. Daily Logs & Field Documentation (On-Site Core)
Daily job logs: Crew sign-in/out, weather notes, work completed, delays/issues, photos/videos attached — auto-timestamped and stored per job.
Punch lists / inspections: Create checklists for walkthroughs, assign fixes to subs or yourself, mark as resolved with before/after photos.
Safety checklists: OSHA-style forms (e.g., toolbox talks, hazard reports) with digital signatures.
Why add it: Prevents disputes, supports change orders, and creates a paper trail for insurance/claims — super useful for general contracting.
3. Time & Crew Management (Labor Tracking)
GPS-verified time clock: Crew clocks in/out via mobile (with location proof), auto-links hours to specific jobs.
Crew scheduling: Calendar view to assign workers/subs to jobs, conflict alerts, availability checker.
Labor cost tracking: Real-time job costing (hours × rate) to see if you're over/under budget mid-project.
Why add it: Helps control payroll, spot inefficiencies, and bill accurately for time-and-materials jobs.
4. Change Orders & Variations
Dedicated change order workflow: Client requests, add costs/labor/time, get approval (digital signature), auto-update job budget/invoice.
Notification chain: Alert subs/crew of changes via push or email.
Why add it: Change orders are a huge profit source (and headache) in contracting — streamlining them reduces lost revenue and arguments.
5. Client Portal / Communication Hub
Client-facing login (separate from sub portal): View job progress, timelines, photos, invoices, pay online, approve change orders, leave feedback.
Automated updates: Send progress photos/notes/SMS (e.g., "Foundation poured today") without manual emailing.
Why add it: Builds trust, reduces "where are we at?" calls, and encourages referrals/repeat business.
6. Inventory & Material Management (If You Stock or Order Often)
Simple tool/material tracker: Log what's used per job, low-stock alerts, reorder lists.
Supplier contacts/quick order forms: Save vendor info for fast quotes.
Why add it: Prevents running out mid-job or over-ordering — especially helpful for smaller GCs handling their own supplies.
7. Analytics & Insights Dashboard (Business Health Overview)
High-level metrics: Monthly revenue, profit per job type, top clients, average job duration, mileage/expense trends.
Alerts: Over-budget jobs, overdue invoices, subs with high YTD payments (1099 flag).
Exportable summaries: For your accountant or loan applications.
Why add it: Gives you a quick "business pulse" without spreadsheets — helps spot growth opportunities or issues early.
8. Integrations & Exports (Bridge to Other Tools)
QuickBooks/Excel/CSV exports for accounting (since you're moving away from ZenBusiness limits).
Google Calendar sync for scheduling.
Stripe/PayPal for in-app payments (clients pay deposits/invoices directly).
Why add it: Lets you keep what works (e.g., banking) while centralizing ops in your app.

If paperwork/delays frustrate you → Daily logs + punch lists first.
If estimating/bidding is time-consuming → Estimating section.
If crew tracking or client updates are issues → Time clock + client portal.
