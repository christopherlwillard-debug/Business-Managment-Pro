The Big Upgrade: Multi-Business Structure

Instead of everything mixed together, every job should belong to:

👉 A Business Profile

* Asphalt
* Contracting
* (future: anything else you add)

⸻

🖥️ Your Dashboard (Now Done Right)

🔁 First: Add a Business Filter

At the top:

* All Businesses
* Asphalt
* Contracting

This lets you:

* View combined totals
* Or isolate each business

⸻

🔝 Core Numbers (Same Logic, Works for Both)

These stay the same no matter the business:

💵 Revenue

All money collected

🧾 Expenses

Only true costs (materials, fuel, etc.)

📈 Net Profit

Business performance

🤝 Subcontractor Owed

Unpaid amounts across jobs

💰 Your Actual Profit

What you actually keep

⸻

🧠 Why This Works

Now you can answer:

* “How is asphalt doing vs contracting?”
* “Which one is more profitable?”
* “Where am I actually making money?”

⸻

📊 Add This (Game-Changer for 2 Businesses)

👉 “Profit by Business” Section

Show something like:

* Asphalt
    * Revenue
    * Profit
    * Sub owed
* Contracting
    * Revenue
    * Profit
    * Sub owed

Now you instantly see which side is stronger.

⸻

📋 Job Structure (Universal for BOTH)

Every job—no matter the business—should follow the same layout:

🧾 Job Info

* Business type (asphalt or contracting)
* Customer
* Total revenue

⸻

💸 Expenses

* Materials
* Labor supplies
* Fuel
    (whatever applies to that job type)

⸻

📈 Profit

* Revenue minus expenses

⸻

🤝 Split

* Subcontractor % or custom amount

⸻

💰 Payout Tracking

* How much they’re owed
* How much is paid
* What’s left

⸻

🔒 Status

* Open
* Needs payout
* Settled

⸻

🔴 Add This Filter (You’ll Use It Daily)

“Jobs Needing Payout”

Across BOTH businesses:

* Shows anything unpaid

This becomes your:

“Who do I owe money to right now?” screen

⸻

🧠 One Important Design Rule

Do NOT build:

* Separate systems for asphalt and contracting

Instead:

Build ONE system that adapts per job

⸻

🔥 Optional (But Really Powerful)

Add “Job Type” Inside Each Business

For example:

Asphalt

* Sealcoating
* Crack fill

Contracting

* Interior finish
* Exterior work
* Repairs

Now later you can see:

* Which services make the most money

⸻

⚖️ Reality Check (This is why you’re thinking ahead right)

As you grow:

* Multiple jobs
* Multiple subs
* Two businesses

Without this structure:
👉 Everything blends together and gets confusing fast

With this:
👉 You’re basically building your own contractor CRM + profit system
