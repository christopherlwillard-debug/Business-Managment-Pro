what could i do to make it the best in app bidding platform i can that will help my business 

Since this app is supposed to help your business win work, I wouldn't try to compete with giant platforms like GovWin, GovDash, or Govly on having the most bid sources. Those companies have huge budgets and already aggregate opportunities from many sources. Instead, build the platform around answering one question:

"Should I pursue this bid, and how do I win it?"

For a small contractor, that's where the value is.

Tier 1: Must-Have Features
AI Bid Scoring

Every opportunity gets a score from 1–100 based on:

Distance from your service area
Contract size
Required licenses
SDVOSB eligibility
Plumbing relevance
Mobile home relevance
Utility hookup relevance
Past projects completed

Example:

Score: 92/100

Excellent fit
SDVOSB eligible
Within 50 miles
Requires plumbing hookups

Most bid platforms stop at showing opportunities. The best ones help users decide whether to bid. Opportunity qualification and bid/no-bid analysis are becoming key features in modern GovCon platforms.

AI Solicitation Reader

Upload a 100-page PDF and get:

Due date
Mandatory site visit date
Insurance requirements
Bonding requirements
Scope summary
Required certifications
Risk warnings

Nobody wants to read a 150-page solicitation manually.

Deadline Dashboard

Show:

Days remaining
Missing documents
Pending questions
Proposal status

Think of it like a CRM for bids.

Tier 2: Features That Actually Help You Win
Agency Intelligence

If an agency posts bids frequently:

Show:

Average award size
Award frequency
Common contractors
Previous winners

This lets users understand who they're competing against. AI tools increasingly use spending and award data to identify procurement patterns and competitor behavior.

Competitor Intelligence

Pull award history and show:

Who usually wins
How often they win
Contract values
Agency relationships

If a company has won 15 of the last 20 contracts, users should know before spending days preparing a proposal.

Teaming Partner Finder

This is huge.

Let users search for:

SDVOSBs
HUBZone firms
8(a) firms
WOSBs
General contractors

Many businesses win their first government work through teaming arrangements rather than direct awards.

Tier 3: What Makes It Better Than Most Platforms
AI Proposal Builder

Upload:

Past proposals
Capability statements
Certifications

Then generate:

Proposal drafts
Executive summaries
Technical responses
Capability statements

AI-assisted proposal generation and compliance checking are becoming major differentiators.

Compliance Matrix Generator

Automatically identify:

Every requirement
Every deliverable
Every attachment
Every certification

Then create a checklist.

Missing one required document can kill a proposal.

Win Probability Engine

Show:

Current Win Probability: 74%

Based on:

Distance
Certifications
Competition
Past performance
Agency history
Bid size

This becomes addictive because users constantly check their score.

The Feature I'd Build First

Because you own a mobile-home finishing and hookup company, I'd build a specialized feature called:

"Project Match"

The AI reads a bid and says:

This bid requires:

Water hookup
Sewer hookup
Plumbing
Skirting
Finish work

You offer:

Water hookups
Sewer hookups
Plumbing
Underpinning
Siding

Match Score: 94%

Then explain exactly why.

That feature directly helps your company find jobs and could later become the core of a subscription product for other contractors.

Long-Term Vision

Instead of:

"A place to find bids"

Build:

"An AI capture manager for contractors."

The highest-value workflow is:

Find opportunity
Analyze opportunity
Score opportunity
Decide bid/no-bid
Build proposal
Track submission
Track award

The platforms gaining traction are moving toward managing that entire lifecycle rather than simply listing opportunities.

For your specific business, I'd start with AI Match Scoring, Solicitation Summaries, Capability Statement Generation, and SDVOSB Opportunity Detection before anything else. Those four features would immediately help you win more work while also making the app something other contractors would pay for.
