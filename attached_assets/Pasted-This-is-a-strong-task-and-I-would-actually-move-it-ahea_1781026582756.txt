This is a strong task and I would actually move it ahead of Theme Designer (#723) and maybe even ahead of Templates (#725).

Why?

Looking at your screenshot, the biggest problem isn't colors or fonts. It's that users see those service cards and immediately think:

"How do I change that icon?"

"How do I make this card look different?"

"Can I use a picture instead?"

"Can I add my SBA logo here?"

Right now the answer is basically "not really."

The Universal Card Engine solves a huge amount of that.
