Task #1416 – Dashboard & Accounting Organization Cleanup

Objective

This task is an organization and usability improvement only.

Do NOT redesign the application.
Do NOT add new APIs.
Do NOT change calculations.
Do NOT change business logic.
Do NOT remove existing functionality.

The goal is to eliminate duplicate information, group related information together, reduce unnecessary scrolling, and make the Dashboard easier to use as the primary business command center.
