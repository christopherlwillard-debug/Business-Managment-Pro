#1287 - Separate "Save Changes" from "Record Payment" on Sub Payment Screen

What & Why

The Sub Payment screen (WorkerPayCard in JobPayroll.tsx) currently conflates two separate business events:

1. Accruing labor cost (the subcontractor has earned money).
2. Recording a cash payment (the subcontractor has actually been paid).

After logging hours, contractors frequently return later to add bonuses, edit hours, adjust travel time, or record advancement deductions. Today, those edits cannot be saved without pressing "Review & Record," which immediately creates accounting transactions and marks the subcontractor as paid.

Labor cost is incurred when work is performed—not when cash leaves the business. Separating these workflows allows contractors to accurately monitor job profitability throughout the life of a project while recording payments only when money actually changes hands.

Done Looks Like

• A primary "Save Changes" button persists all editable payroll information without recording a payment.

Saving updates:
- Work hours
- Travel hours
- Work rate
- Travel rate
- Bonus
- Bonus note
- Advancement deduction
- Payment method draft
- Payment reference draft

Saving:
- Creates NO ledger transaction.
- Creates NO payment record.
- Does NOT change payroll status.
- Shows a success toast.
- Clears the dirty/unsaved state.

• Job costing uses accrued labor instead of recorded payments.

Immediately after saving:
- Gross Earned is recalculated.
- Outstanding Pay is recalculated.
- The Job Card immediately updates:
  - Labor Cost
  - Total Job Cost
  - Projected Profit
  - Projected Profit Margin

Job profitability must always use accrued labor regardless of whether payment has been recorded.

Recording a payment only affects:
- Cash movement
- Payroll history
- Payment ledger
- Outstanding balance

Recording a payment must NEVER change labor cost or job profitability.

• Add a worker earnings summary near the top of the card.

Display:

Gross Earned
(Hours + Travel + Bonus)

Already Paid
(Sum of all recorded payments)

Outstanding
(Gross Earned - Already Paid)

These values update immediately after Save Changes.

• Add a payment status badge.

Display one of the following on every worker card:

🟡 Draft
Saved labor exists but no payment has been recorded.

🟠 Partially Paid
Some payments have been recorded and Outstanding > $0.

🟢 Paid in Full
Outstanding = $0.

• Separate payment recording from editing.

Rename the existing button to:

Review & Record Payment — $X

Move it into a sticky bottom action bar that is always visible.

Only display it when:
- Hourly subcontractor
- Not fully paid
- Log Hours panel is closed
- Outstanding balance > $0

Selecting this button opens the existing Review & Record confirmation panel.

After confirmation:
- Create payment ledger entry.
- Record payment.
- Update Already Paid.
- Update Outstanding.
- Update payroll status.

No other behavior should change.

• Protect against unsaved changes.

Editing any payroll field marks the form as dirty.

Dirty fields include:
- Work hours
- Travel hours
- Work rate
- Travel rate
- Bonus
- Bonus note
- Advancement deduction
- Payment method
- Payment reference

If the user attempts to leave the page with unsaved changes, display:

"You have unsaved payroll changes."

Options:
- Save Changes
- Discard
- Cancel

After Save Changes completes successfully, clear the dirty state.

Out of Scope

- Changing the payment confirmation workflow.
- Fixed or Allocation subcontractors.
- Redesigning payment history.
- Redesigning the accounting ledger.
- Payroll reporting.

- Bonus

- Advancement deduction
- Payment method
- Payment reference

Saving draft data must NOT create ledger entries or payment records.

2. Add Save Changes.

Place a primary "Save Changes" button below the Earnings / Bonus / Advancement section.

On click:
- Save draft payroll data.
- Show success toast.
- Remain on page.
- Do not advance payroll status.

3. Compute accrued earnings.

Immediately recompute:
- Gross Earned
- Already Paid
- Outstanding

Ensure Job Card labor cost and profitability use accrued earnings instead of recorded payments.

4. Add worker summary.

Display:
- Gross Earned
- Already Paid
- Outstanding

near the top of the worker card.

5. Add payment status badge.

Derive status from:
- Outstanding balance
- Recorded payments
- Draft payroll data

Statuses:
- Draft
- Partially Paid
- Paid in Full

6. Add sticky payment action.

Rename:
Review & Record Payment — $X

Move into a sticky bottom action bar with iOS safe-area padding.

Remove the existing inline Review & Record button.

7. Add dirty-state protection.

Track whether payroll fields have unsaved edits.

Prompt before leaving the page.

Clear dirty flag after Save Changes completes successfully.

8. Separate accounting concepts.

Maintain two independent concepts throughout the payroll system.

Accrued Labor
- Job Costing
- Labor Cost
- Gross Earned
- Outstanding Balance
- Projected Profitability

Recorded Payments
- Cash Movement
- Payment Ledger
- Payroll History
- Bank Reconciliation
- Already Paid

Never derive accrued labor from recorded payments, and never change labor cost when recording a payment.
