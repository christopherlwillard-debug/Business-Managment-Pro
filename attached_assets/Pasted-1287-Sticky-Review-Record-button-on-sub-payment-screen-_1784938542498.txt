1287 - Sticky "Review & Record" button on sub payment screen

What & Why

On the Sub Payment screen (JobPayroll.tsx, WorkerPayCard), the "Review & Record — $X" button lives at the very bottom of a long scrollable card. Once a sub has logged entries the card grows tall (Earnings → Logged Entries → Bonus → Advancement → Payment Info → button). Entering a bonus makes the card even taller, pushing the button completely off-screen with no visual hint that it exists. The user stops at the bonus fields and thinks there is no way to save the payment.

Done looks like

A sticky action bar appears fixed to the bottom of the viewport whenever the hourly payment form is open (hours > 0, not fully paid, not in the Log Hours sub-panel). It shows "Review & Record — $X" exactly as the existing button does.
The button is disabled (greyed out) when netToPay <= 0, matching current behaviour.
The original button at the bottom of the card is removed (or hidden) so there is no duplicate.
Scrolling the card still works normally; only the button is pinned.
On larger screens the sticky bar still renders (it doesn't hurt desktop layout and keeps the UX consistent).
Out of scope

Changing how the Review panel itself works.
Adding a sticky bar for Fixed / Allocation subs (those are one-field forms, short enough to see the button).
Steps

Add a sticky bottom bar — render a fixed bottom-0 left-0 right-0 bar (with safe-area inset padding for iOS) that contains the "Review & Record" button. Gate it on isHourly && !isFullyPaid && !showLogHours && (parseFloat(workHours) || 0) > 0.
Remove the inline button — delete (or conditionally hide with isHourly) the existing "Review & Record" button at the bottom of the card to avoid duplication; keep the "+ Log Hours" secondary link where it is.
Ensure the sticky bar clears the last card — add pb-24 (or equivalent) bottom padding to the page's scroll container so the last card's content isn't hidden behind the sticky bar.
