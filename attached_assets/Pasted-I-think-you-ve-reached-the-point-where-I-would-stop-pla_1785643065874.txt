#1473 – Smart Alignment & Snapping

Score: 9.8/10

This is exactly the kind of feature users don't notice consciously, but they feel immediately.

The only thing I'd change:
1. Don't compute against every component.

Right now it says:

Compare against all sibling components.

That sounds fine...

...until a page has:

300 components
12 sections
dozens of images

Then you're doing hundreds of comparisons on every mouse move.

Instead I'd say:

Performance Requirement
Guide calculations should prioritize nearby siblings.

Do not compare against every component on every drag frame.

Use spatial filtering (same parent/container first, then nearby siblings) and throttle calculations to animation frames (requestAnimationFrame).

Maintain smooth 60 FPS dragging on pages with 200+ components.

That one paragraph will prevent future performance issues.

2. Make snapping optional with Alt

Every design tool does this.

I'd add:

Holding Alt temporarily disables snapping while dragging.

Very useful.

3. Add a "magnetic" feeling

Instead of:

Snap instantly.

Use:

When entering the snap threshold, ease into alignment over the final few pixels.

The interaction should feel magnetic rather than abrupt.

Tiny detail.

Huge UX difference.

4. History

I'd add this:

Alignment guides and snapping must not generate additional undo entries.

Dragging a snapped object remains a single undo action.

Very important.
