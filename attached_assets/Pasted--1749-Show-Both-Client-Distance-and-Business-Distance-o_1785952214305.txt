#1749 - Show Both Client Distance and Business Distance on Every Job Card

Background

Every job has TWO completely independent distance calculations that serve different business purposes.

Client Distance
Origin:
Client address (Clayton, Cavco, Champion, homeowner, etc.)

Destination:
Job site

Purpose:
Mileage surcharge eligibility and customer billing.

Business Distance
Origin:
Business address (2504 Krauss Ct)

Destination:
Job site

Purpose:
Operational travel distance, estimated fuel cost, and profitability analysis.

These calculations are independent and must never overwrite or replace each other.
