One feature I'd steal from Figma

Double-click behavior.

Single-click selects the section (Hero, Services, Footer).
Double-click drills into the section (Service Card, Logo, Heading).
Triple-click edits the text directly.

That gives users a predictable way to navigate complex layouts without constantly reaching for the sidebar.

Overall

If I had to summarize the redesign in one sentence, it would be:

The canvas should become the editor. The right panel should become the inspector.

That's the shift that makes tools like Figma, Framer, and Canva feel so natural. Right now your right panel is carrying too much responsibility, and moving most of that interaction onto the canvas would make BuildPro feel significantly more modern and approachable.
