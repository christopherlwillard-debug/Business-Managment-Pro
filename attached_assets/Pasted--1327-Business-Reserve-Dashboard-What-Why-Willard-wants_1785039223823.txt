#1327 - Business Reserve Dashboard

## What & Why

Willard wants one number on his Accounting page that answers:

"How much money in my business checking account is already spoken for?"

Today the information already exists throughout the Accounting module:

• Tax Reserve
• Growth Fund
• Recurring Expenses
• Plaid Bank Balance
• Estimated Tax Liability

This feature combines those existing calculations into a single Business Reserve Dashboard that becomes the primary cash planning widget for the Accounting Overview page.

The dashboard is intended for cash management—not accounting—and should clearly separate actual reserved cash from estimated tax liability.

---

## Done Looks Like

A new "Business Reserve Dashboard" card appears at the top of Accounting → Overview above the existing Tax & Growth Reserve section.

The dashboard displays:

KEEP IN BUSINESS CHECKING

$X,XXX.XX

This number represents the minimum amount that should remain in the business checking account today.

The total is calculated as:

Required Reserve =
Tax Reserve
+ Available Growth Fund
+ Next 30 Days of Recurring Expenses
+ Operating Reserve

The dashboard displays four clickable breakdown cards:

• Tax Reserve
• Growth Fund
• Business Overhead (30 Days)
• Operating Reserve

Each card shows its contribution to the Required Reserve and navigates or scrolls to the existing detail section already in the Accounting page.

If a Plaid checking account is connected, the dashboard also displays:

Available to Spend

Business Checking Balance
− Required Reserve

If positive:

Display in green.

If negative:

Display:

Short Reserve

in red.

A Reserve Health badge is shown.

Green
Fully Funded

Yellow
Reserve Getting Low

Red
Below Required Reserve

If no Plaid account exists:

Hide the health indicator and instead display:

"Connect your business bank account to calculate available cash."

Display an Overhead Coverage statistic showing:

Days of Overhead

or

Months of Overhead

Formula:

Required Reserve Available For Overhead
÷
Monthly Recurring Expenses

The Operating Reserve allows selecting:

• None
• 1 Month
• 2 Months
• 3 Months
• Custom Dollar Amount

The setting is persisted per business.

The dashboard must clearly distinguish:

Estimated Tax Liability

vs

Tax Reserve

Estimated Tax Liability is accounting information only.

Tax Reserve represents actual cash intentionally set aside.

Only Tax Reserve contributes to the Required Reserve calculation.

## Out of Scope

Changing Tax Reserve calculations

Changing Growth Fund calculations

Changing recurring expense calculations

Historical reserve graphs

Multi-business consolidation

Reserve enforcement or spending locks

---

## Backend

Create:

GET /api/reports/business-reserve

Return a fully calculated object:

{
  taxReserve,
  growthFundAvailable,
  recurring30DayReserve,
  monthlyRecurring,
  operatingReserve,
  requiredReserve,
  checkingBalance,
  availableToSpend,
  reserveHealth,
  overheadCoverageDays,
  overheadCoverageMonths,
  plaidConnected
}

The backend should calculate the final Required Reserve instead of requiring the frontend to add values together.

This ensures one source of truth for every future screen.

## Design Principles

• One source of truth for business cash planning.
• Cash management and accounting remain separate concepts.
• Estimated Tax Liability is informational only.
• Required Reserve always represents actual dollars that should remain in the checking account.
• Every value displayed on the dashboard comes from existing accounting calculations—no duplicate business logic should exist in the frontend.
