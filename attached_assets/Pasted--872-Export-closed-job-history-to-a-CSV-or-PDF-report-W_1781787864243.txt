#872 - Export closed job history to a CSV or PDF report
What & Why
The Closed Jobs archive table shows all settled jobs with revenue and profit totals, but there's no way to export that data for accounting, tax prep, or sharing with stakeholders. An export button would let Willard pull a year-end summary or hand records to a bookkeeper.

Done looks like
An "Export" button appears in the Closed Jobs section header
Clicking it downloads a CSV (or PDF) with one row per closed job: date closed, job title, customer, location, revenue, net profit, invoice status, sub status
Totals row at the bottom (revenue, profit)
Filename includes the current date and optional business-line filter

Yes, absolutely.

For your accountant, #873 is actually more important than #872.

Think about it this way:

#871 = Organization

Moves finished jobs out of the way.

#872 = Export

Lets you download the data.

#873 = Accounting Accuracy

Makes sure the data is actually included in your financial statements.

If #873 is missing, your P&L could look like:

Revenue: $80,000
Expenses: $35,000
Profit: $45,000

when you've actually closed another $40,000 worth of jobs.

That's a huge problem.
