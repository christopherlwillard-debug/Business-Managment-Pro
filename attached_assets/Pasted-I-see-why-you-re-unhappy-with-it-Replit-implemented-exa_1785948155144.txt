I think you just identified why the feature felt wrong. There are actually two completely different mileage systems, and they should never be mixed because they answer different business questions.

1. Client Mileage (Billing)

From: Clayton Plant → Job Site

Purpose:

Determine if a mileage surcharge should be billed.
Used for customer billing only.

Example:

Clayton Evansville
        ↓
Job Site

62.4 miles

Mileage Surcharge
✓ Applies

This has nothing to do with your truck or your operating costs.

2. Business Mileage (Operations)

From: Your Business Address → Job Site

Purpose:

Estimate fuel cost.
Estimate operational profitability.
Never affects invoices.

Example:

Willard's General Contracting
        ↓
Job Site

41.7 miles

Estimated Fuel Cost
$18.92

This has nothing to do with Clayton's surcharge.
