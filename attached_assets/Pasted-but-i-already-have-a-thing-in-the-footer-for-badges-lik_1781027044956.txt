I agree. If your goal is to build a website builder for all service businesses, I'd keep the Certification Card preset in #806.

The key is not making it a separate card type. It should just be a preset of the Universal Card.

That way:

Universal Card
├─ Service Card
├─ Feature Card
├─ Team Card
├─ Pricing Card
├─ Portfolio Card
├─ Process Card
└─ Certification Card

All use the same underlying CardComponent.

Why I'd keep it

Contractors may want:

Veteran Owned
SBA Certified
Licensed & Insured
Bonded
BBB Accredited

displayed:

In the footer
Under the hero banner
In an About section
In a Certifications section
In a side-by-side row with services
On a landing page

A footer-only badge system becomes restrictive.

Even better

I'd slightly rename it:

Trust Card

instead of

Certification Card

Then it covers:

Veteran Owned

24/7 Emergency Service

Long-term

You can still keep your existing footer badge manager.

Think of it this way:

Badge Manager = global certifications/trust assets
Universal Card Engine = where those assets can be displayed anywhere

So a user could upload their SBA logo once into the Media Library, then drop it into:

Footer
Hero trust strip
About page
Certification card
Sidebar CTA

without re-uploading it.

That gives you maximum flexibility without creating a second card architecture.
