Suggested Task #1442 (revised)

What & Why

Willard needs to record context on a business trip that doesn't fit any existing field—for example:

Trip was not approved for billing.
Client cancelled after arrival.
Warranty work (non-billable).
Waiting on customer approval.
Additional work sold while on site.

Currently there is no place to save this information, so important tax and business context is lost.

Done looks like
✅ A Notes textarea appears on the Create Business Trip form.
✅ The same Notes field appears when editing an existing trip.
✅ Notes are optional.
✅ Notes are persisted in the database.
✅ Notes are returned from the API.
✅ Notes are displayed in the expanded Business Trip card in their own Trip Notes section.
✅ Trips with empty notes display nothing extra.
✅ Existing trips continue working without migration issues.
Out of scope
Receipt-level notes (already handled by receipt description)
PDF audit packet inclusion
Searching/filtering by notes
Rich text formatting
