That's exactly how I'd think about it.

If you're already checking:

Your bank app
Credit union app
Business banking app

for the actual live balance, then BuildPro doesn't need to be a real-time banking dashboard.

The value of the Plaid integration in BuildPro is probably:

✅ Automatically importing transactions

✅ Categorizing expenses

✅ Job costing

✅ P&L tracking

✅ Cash flow reporting

✅ Reconciliation

Not:

❌ Replacing your banking app

For your use case, I'd almost be tempted to do:

Balance refresh = once per day

or even:

Balance refresh only when Sync is clicked

Then show:

Balance
$12,847.23

Last Updated:
June 17, 2026 8:00 AM

That gives you a reasonable estimate of cash on hand without constantly paying for balance refreshes.

If I were designing BuildPro strictly for Willard's General Contracting, I'd care much more about:

"How much did I spend on Job A?"
"How much did I spend at Lowe's this month?"
"What's my profit this quarter?"
"Which invoices are unpaid?"

than whether the bank balance is accurate to the minute.

In fact, if Plaid costs become annoying, you could even make the balance card secondary and treat the banking page primarily as a transaction feed and accounting tool, since that's where the real value is for managing your business.
