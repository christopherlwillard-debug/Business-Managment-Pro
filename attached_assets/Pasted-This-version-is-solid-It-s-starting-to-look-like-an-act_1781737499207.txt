This version is solid. It's starting to look like an actual accounts payable system instead of just a job calculator.

A few things I especially like:

1. Actual Amount Owed Override

This is the biggest improvement.

Real life:

Calculated Allocation: $617.50

But you tell James:

"I'll give you $700 on this one."

Now:

Calculated Allocation: $617.50
Actual Amount Owed: $700.00

Everything should calculate from the Actual Amount Owed.

2. Partial Status

This is how it should work:

Amount Owed: $700
Paid: $0
Status: Unpaid

Then:

Amount Owed: $700
Paid: $500
Balance: $200
Status: Partial

Then:

Amount Owed: $700
Paid: $700
Balance: $0
Status: Paid

Very clean.

3. Outstanding Balances Page

This is probably going to become one of your most-used screens.

Example:

Outstanding Sub Balances

Dewayne      $450
James        $200
Mike         $700

Total Outstanding
$1,350

That's an instant answer to:

"How much cash do I need to clear everybody out today?"

One Thing I'd Still Add

I would add another summary number:

Outstanding Sub Balances

Dewayne      $450
James        $200
Mike         $700

Total Outstanding: $1,350

Jobs Impacted: 3
Subs Owed: 3

Because later you'll have enough jobs running that you'll want to know both.

Another Future-Proofing Change

I'd ask them to rename:

Amount Owed

to

Agreed Amount Owed

because that's what it really is.

You might have:

Calculated Allocation: $617.50
Agreed Amount Owed: $700.00

which makes it crystal clear that the number came from an agreement, not the formula.

One More Thing To Consider

For your manufactured-home work, I would eventually add a checkbox:

☐ Hold Until Customer Pays

Example:

Customer hasn't paid dealer invoice yet
You don't want the app screaming that James is owed money tomorrow

The balance would still exist, but you'd know it's intentionally waiting on customer payment.

Not needed right now, but it's something contractors run into a lot.

Overall, I'd approve #864 as written. The only tweak I'd make today is changing "Actual Amount Owed" to "Agreed Amount Owed" because that's a better description of what you're really tracking.
