I would actually replace Task #1287 with this

#1287 - Separate “Save Changes” from “Record Payment”

What & Why

The current Sub Payment screen conflates editing payroll information with recording an actual payment.

After logging hours, users often return later to:

* add bonuses
* adjust hours
* edit notes
* add advancement deductions

Currently these edits cannot be saved without pressing Review & Record, which immediately records a payment in the accounting ledger.

This forces users to either:

* record payroll before they’ve actually paid the worker, or
* avoid tracking labor costs until payday.

For contractors, labor should be visible in the job immediately, while actual cash payments should only occur when money changes hands.

Done Looks Like

* Add a primary Save Changes button that saves the WorkerPayCard draft without recording payment.
* Saving updates:
    * logged hours
    * travel
    * bonus
    * bonus note
    * advancement deduction
    * payment draft fields
* Saving recalculates Gross Earned and Outstanding Pay immediately.
* Job profitability reflects the updated labor cost after Save.
* No accounting transaction or payment ledger entry is created.
* The sticky bottom action becomes Review & Record Payment — $X and is only used when actually paying the subcontractor.
* Review & Record creates the payment ledger entry and marks the amount as paid.

Benefits

* Hours can be logged daily.
* Bonuses can be added later.
* Labor cost appears on the Job Card immediately.
* Cash isn’t recorded until payday.
* Eliminates accidental payroll recordings.
* Matches how contractors actually manage jobs in the field.

⸻

I actually think this is a much stronger design than simply making the Review & Record button sticky. The sticky button is still a good improvement, but separating Save Changes from Record Payment solves the underlying workflow problem instead of just making the wrong button easier to reach.
