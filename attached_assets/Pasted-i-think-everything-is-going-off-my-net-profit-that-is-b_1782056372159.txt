The part that worries me

The Tax & Growth page appears to be using:

Owner share
Tax reserve
Growth fund
Take-home

based on profit calculations that may not be subtracting unpaid sub obligations.

If that's true, then the app is doing:

Revenue
- Recorded expenses
= Net Profit

instead of:

Revenue
- Recorded expenses
- Outstanding subcontractor obligations
= Real economic profit

And for your business model, that's a huge difference.

Example

Say:

Collected: $10,000

Expenses paid: $2,000

Subs still owed: $3,000

Accounting profit:

$10,000 - $2,000 = $8,000

Economic reality:

$10,000
- $2,000
- $3,000 owed to subs
= $5,000

If tax reserve, growth reserve, owner draw, and take-home are calculated from $8,000 instead of $5,000, every number becomes inflated.

The question I'd answer before changing anything

You need to determine:

Is the Tax & Growth page using "Net Profit" or "Actual Profit"?

Because your dashboard is already calculating both.

If Tax & Growth is based on:

Net Profit = $2,933.14

instead of:

Actual Profit
= Net Profit - unpaid subs

then yes, I would consider that a logic bug.

What I would want the app to show

Three distinct concepts:

Operational Profit
$2,434.08

Sub Obligations Outstanding
($1,520.00)

Owner Profit Available
$914.08

Then calculate:

Tax Reserve
Growth Fund
Owner Take-Home

from Owner Profit Available, not Operational Profit.

Because money owed to Dewayne, James, or any other sub is not your profit. It's already committed.

From what I can see in the screenshots, your dashboard seems to understand that distinction, but I'm not convinced the Tax & Growth page does. That's the area I'd investigate next before making any more payment-related changes.
