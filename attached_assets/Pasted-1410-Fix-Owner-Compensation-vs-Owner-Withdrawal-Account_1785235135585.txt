1410 - Fix Owner Compensation vs. Owner Withdrawal Accounting

What & Why

The Job Financials engine already calculates owner compensation correctly using the established waterfall:

Revenue
→ Expenses
→ 5% Business Growth
→ Hourly Employee/Subcontractor Pay
→ 50/50 Profit Split
→ My Share
→ Tax Set-Aside
→ My Take-Home

This calculation is correct and must not change.

The problem occurs after a job is finalized. The accounting engine currently records earned Take-Home as an "Owner Draw" expense, incorrectly treating compensation earned from completed jobs as an equity withdrawal.

This causes duplicate accounting because the same money appears in multiple places:

• Owner Capital
• My Pay
• Take-Home Dashboard
• Owner Withdrawals

The accounting engine must separate these into two completely different concepts:

Owner Compensation (Owner Job Pay)
Money earned from completing jobs. Tracks Earned, Paid, and Remaining Owed. Never reduces Gross Profit, Net Profit, Business Equity, or Owner Capital a second time.

Owner Withdrawal
A rare manual equity withdrawal that is not connected to any completed job.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Single Source of Truth

The Job Financials calculation is the authoritative source for every Owner Compensation amount.

Owner Compensation should never be independently calculated anywhere else in the application.

Every report, dashboard, My Pay summary, Take-Home Dashboard, payroll summary, and Owner Compensation history must reference the same Owner Compensation records generated from Job Financials.

No duplicate calculations should exist anywhere in the application.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Owner Compensation Architecture

Each completed job must generate exactly one Owner Compensation record.

That record represents the owner's earned compensation for that completed job.

Payments made to the owner should reference that Owner Compensation record instead of creating additional compensation records.

Example

Job #42

Owner Compensation
Earned: $1,250

Payments

Payment #1
$500

Payment #2
$500

Payment #3
$250

Remaining Owed
$0

Status
Paid

A completed job should never generate multiple Owner Compensation records unless an administrator intentionally rebuilds accounting data.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Owner Compensation Record

Each Owner Compensation record should contain:

• Job Number
• Job Name
• Completion Date
• Earned Amount
• Amount Paid
• Remaining Balance
• Payment Date(s)
• Notes
• Status

Status values:

• Earned
• Partially Paid
• Paid

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Owner Capital Rules

Owner Capital must remain completely independent from Owner Compensation.

Owner Capital may ONLY change from:

• Capital Contributions
• Capital Returns

Owner Capital must never be affected by:

• Owner Compensation
• Owner Withdrawals
• Revenue
• Job Profit
• Take-Home
• My Pay
• Payroll

Owner Capital continues reading only from owner_capital_entries.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Historical Data Migration

Perform a one-time migration as part of this update.

Every historical expense record where:

category = 'Owner Draw'

should become

category = 'Owner Compensation'

Only update the category.

Do NOT modify:

• Amount
• Date
• Description
• Linked Job
• Notes
• Payment Status
• Created Date
• Updated Date

Safety Rules

Before migrating:

• Confirm the record is associated with a completed job.
• Confirm it was created through the Record Owner Draw workflow.
• Skip any record not linked to a completed job.

No true Owner Equity Withdrawals currently exist, so all existing Owner Draw records are expected to become Owner Compensation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Migration Safety

Before creating or migrating an Owner Compensation record:

Verify the completed job does not already have one.

If an Owner Compensation record already exists:

• Do not create a duplicate.
• Link historical payment records to the existing Owner Compensation record whenever possible.
• Log duplicate records for manual review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Historical Validation

After migration completes:

Run a validation pass against every completed job.

For every completed job:

1. Read the stored Job Financials.

2. Recalculate the expected Owner Compensation using:

• Revenue
• Expenses
• Business Growth
• Hourly Labor
• Profit Split
• Tax Reserve
• Final Take-Home

3. Compare the recalculated value against the migrated Owner Compensation record.

If the values match:

• Mark the record as Migration Verified.

If they do not match:

• Do NOT automatically overwrite anything.
• Flag the record for review.
• Log:

- Job ID
- Migrated Amount
- Expected Amount
- Variance

Expose validation results through the existing Admin Migration page or a new read-only Admin Validation page.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Owner Compensation Rules

Owner Compensation exists only to track:

• Amount Earned
• Amount Paid
• Remaining Balance

Recording Owner Compensation must NEVER:

• Reduce Gross Profit
• Reduce Net Profit
• Reduce Business Equity
• Reduce Owner Capital
• Be treated as an operating expense a second time
• Create duplicate accounting entries

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Owner Withdrawals

Owner Withdrawals become a completely separate manual accounting feature.

Purpose:

Manual removal of business equity that is NOT connected to completed jobs.

Example:

The business has accumulated excess retained earnings.

I manually transfer $5,000 to myself that is unrelated to any completed job.

That transaction is an Owner Withdrawal.

Completed jobs should NEVER automatically generate Owner Withdrawal transactions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Done Looks Like

✓ Job Financials waterfall remains exactly unchanged.

✓ Every completed job generates exactly one Owner Compensation record.

✓ Historical Owner Draw records are migrated to Owner Compensation.

✓ Historical migration validates every completed job against Job Financials.

✓ Duplicate Owner Compensation records cannot be created.

✓ Completed jobs generate Owner Compensation instead of Owner Draw going forward.

✓ Owner Compensation only tracks Earned, Paid, and Remaining Owed.

✓ Owner Compensation never reduces profit or Owner Capital a second time.

✓ Owner Capital continues reading only from owner_capital_entries.

✓ My Pay becomes the single source of truth for Owner Compensation.

✓ Take-Home Dashboard summarizes the My Pay ledger by month instead of performing its own calculation.

✓ Record Owner Draw buttons become Record Owner Pay.

✓ Dashboard Record Withdrawal becomes Record Owner Pay.

✓ A separate Record Equity Withdrawal button is available for true manual equity withdrawals.

✓ Recent Withdrawals becomes Recent Owner Pay.

✓ Owner Withdrawals are empty after migration unless true manual equity withdrawals are recorded in the future.

✓ No historical compensation data is lost.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Out of Scope

• Changing the Job Financials waterfall.
• Changing Revenue calculations.
• Changing Expense calculations.
• Changing Business Growth calculations.
• Changing Profit Split calculations.
• Changing Tax calculations.
• Automatically correcting validation failures (manual review only).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Implementation Steps

1. Data Migration

Run a one-time migration:

UPDATE expenses
SET category = 'Owner Compensation'
WHERE category = 'Owner Draw';

Only migrate records linked to completed jobs.

Prevent duplicate Owner Compensation records.

2. Historical Validation

After migration:

Iterate every completed job.

Recalculate Owner Compensation using Job Financials.

Compare against migrated records.

Log mismatches.

Expose validation results through an Admin page.

3. API

Update owner-pay-summary to read only:

category = 'Owner Compensation'

Update POST /api/jobs/:id/record-owner-draw

Rename internally to Owner Compensation while preserving the endpoint if needed for backward compatibility.

4. Jobs UI

Rename:

Record Owner Draw

to

Record Owner Pay

Update request payloads to use Owner Compensation.

5. Job Payroll UI

Rename:

Record Owner Draw

to

Record Owner Pay

Update payload category.

6. Dashboard UI

Rename:

Record Withdrawal

to

Record Owner Pay

Update payload category.

Add:

Record Equity Withdrawal

which records category:

Owner Withdrawal

7. Dashboard Copy

Rename:

Recent Withdrawals

to

Recent Owner Pay

Update dialog descriptions accordingly.

8. Accounting Overview

Rewrite the Take-Home Dashboard.

Instead of calculating projected values independently, summarize Owner Compensation records by month directly from the My Pay ledger.

This removes duplicate calculations and guarantees every report displays identical owner compensation values.
