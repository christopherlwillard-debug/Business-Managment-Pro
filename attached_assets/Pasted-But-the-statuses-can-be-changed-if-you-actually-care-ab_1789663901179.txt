But the statuses can be changed if you actually care about tracking them separately.

The important distinction:

Job status ≠ component status.

You can be working on the job without having to manually set every component.

5. Most importantly: don’t make “Here” a scheduling concept

The user shouldn’t have to think:

“Was I supposed to be here?”
“Is this an unrecorded day?”
“Is this a calendar day or production day?”

They know one thing:

I worked today.

So the app asks:

Did you work today?

YES — WE WORKED HERE

That’s the granny test.

⸻

And I would not automatically change every component to “In Progress” when you start the job. Keep component statuses independent because you may have a job where you’re working today but only one component is actually underway.

The hierarchy should be:

The app handles the complicated accounting/scheduling logic underneath. You should only have to tell it what you actually did.

That is the direction I’d take before adding another scheduling feature.
