Long-term benefit

If every earning, advance, and payment creates a ledger entry, then at year-end you can instantly answer questions like:

How much have I paid this subcontractor this year?
What jobs did they work on?
How much is still owed?
How much did I front them in fuel, meals, or tools?

That same ledger also becomes the foundation for generating a 1099 payment summary or reconciling your records. You're not just building a payment screen—you'd be building a complete financial history for each subcontractor, with one source of truth that both you and the subcontractor can reference.
