One final suggestion

I would add one more item to the "Done looks like" section:

The inspector can be pinned or unpinned.

Unpinned (default): Opens temporarily over the canvas and closes with Esc or by clicking outside.
Pinned: Behaves like the traditional persistent inspector for users who prefer that workflow. The pinned state is remembered per user.

That gives you the best of both worlds. New users get a clean, modern, canvas-focused experience, while experienced users who like having the inspector always visible can opt into that workflow without forcing it on everyone. I think that's the strongest long-term design because it doesn't take functionality away—it simply makes the modern workflow the default.
