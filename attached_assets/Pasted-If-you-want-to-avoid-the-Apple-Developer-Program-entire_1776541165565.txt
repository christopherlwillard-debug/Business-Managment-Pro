If you want to avoid the Apple Developer Program entirely (no $99/year fee, no entitlement requests, no TestFlight builds), then Tap to Pay on iPhone is not possible for your BuildPro Mobile app.
Why It’s Not Possible Without Apple Developer
•  Tap to Pay on iPhone uses Apple’s NFC hardware on the iPhone.
•  Apple strictly requires the Tap to Pay entitlement (development + publishing) for any app that wants to use the iPhone as a contactless reader.
•  This entitlement process is only available through an Apple Developer account.
•  You also need a custom development build (not Expo Go), because Expo Go does not support the native NFC access required for Terminal/Tap to Pay.
•  There is no workaround or web-based way to enable it — it’s a hard Apple platform restriction.
Skipping the Apple side means you cannot turn your iPhone into a card reader via software alone.
What You Can Do Instead While Keeping Everything in Replit
Here are your realistic options, ordered from easiest to more involved, all while staying inside Replit (no native iOS builds required):
1.  Use Your Existing Physical Livemode Reader (Recommended for In-Person Payments)
	•  This is the hardware reader shown in your Stripe dashboard screenshot (with the long serial number).
	•  You do not need Apple Developer or any mobile app changes for this.
	•  Best approach for a Replit web app: Use server-driven integration (Stripe recommends this for smart readers).
		•  Your Replit backend (Node.js/Python) creates a Connection Token and PaymentIntent via the Stripe API.
		•  The physical reader connects directly to Stripe over the internet (Wi-Fi or cellular).
		•  Your web app tells Stripe “send this payment to reader ID XXX”.
	•  In your Replit project:
		•  Install the stripe package.
		•  Add a simple backend route to generate the connection token.
		•  Trigger payments from your frontend (e.g., a “Charge Customer” button that calls your Replit server).
	•  Your reader is already registered in livemode — just make sure it’s powered on, connected to Wi-Fi (use admin passcode 07139 if needed to set it up), and shows as online in the Stripe dashboard.
2.  Stick to Online/Card-Not-Present Payments in Your Replit App
	•  Use Stripe Checkout or Payment Element — fully web-based, works in any browser (including on your phone or iPad).
	•  Customer enters card details manually or uses saved cards/Apple Pay on web (Safari).
	•  This is the simplest Replit-friendly option: Replit has built-in tools/agents that can add Stripe Checkout for you very quickly.
	•  No hardware or NFC needed.
3.  Use a Third-Party App for Tap to Pay (No Custom Coding)
	•  Download a ready-made app from the App Store that already has Tap to Pay enabled with Stripe (examples include apps like “3leaf Payment Terminal” or similar Stripe-integrated POS apps).
	•  Link it to your same Stripe account.
	•  This lets you accept contactless payments on your iPhone without building anything yourself.
	•  Downside: It’s not integrated into your BuildPro app — you’d switch between apps.
4.  Android Alternative (If You Switch Devices)
	•  Tap to Pay on Android has much lighter requirements and often doesn’t need the same level of custom entitlements.
	•  If you have an Android phone, it might be easier to enable via the Stripe Terminal SDK on a React Native build.
Quick Recommendation for You
Since you already have a physical reader registered in your Stripe dashboard, the fastest path forward is to integrate that hardware with your Replit web app using server-driven Stripe Terminal API calls. This gives you in-person payments (insert/tap/swipe) without touching Apple’s restrictions at all.
