I think there's one more thing to clarify in your app

You mentioned:

"what I'm paid is different than what I have taken from what I have put in."

That's an important distinction. In accounting there are really three separate buckets:

Capital Contributions – Money you personally invest into the business.
Owner Pay/Draws/Distributions – Money you take out of the business.
Business Profit (Equity) – The accumulated profit the business has earned.

Those three values should be tracked independently and related by accounting rules—not by duplicating transactions across multiple screens. I think BuildPro will be much easier to understand if each page answers one question instead of trying to answer all three.
