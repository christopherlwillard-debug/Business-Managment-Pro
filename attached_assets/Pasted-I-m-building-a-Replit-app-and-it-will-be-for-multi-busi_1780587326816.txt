That’s actually a much stronger approach than trying to force every business into the same workflow.

What you’re really building is a business operating system where each LLC, DBA, or company is its own workspace, and each workspace has its own tool stack.

Architecture I’d Recommend

Company Level

When a user creates a business:

Example:

* Willard’s General Contracting
* Willard’s Asphalt Sealing
* Willard Properties LLC

Each gets:

* Separate CRM
* Separate customers
* Separate jobs
* Separate estimates
* Separate invoices
* Separate documents
* Separate branding
* Separate reports

Then at the owner level:

Owner Dashboard

You can see:

Combined View

* Total Revenue
* Total Outstanding Invoices
* Total Leads
* Total Jobs

Or filter:

* General Contracting only
* Asphalt only
* Rentals only

Think of it like having multiple companies under one parent account.

For Your Replit Prompt

I’d tell the AI something like:

Build a multi-company SaaS platform where each company operates in a completely isolated workspace. Every workspace has separate customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, files, settings, and reports. The platform should use a modular tool system where tools can be enabled or disabled per company. The account owner can optionally view consolidated financials and analytics across all companies while maintaining complete operational separation between businesses. Include support for industry templates that automatically enable relevant tool modules during company creation.

That gives you a scalable foundation where you’re not building one giant contractor app—you’re building a platform that can grow into dozens of business types.
