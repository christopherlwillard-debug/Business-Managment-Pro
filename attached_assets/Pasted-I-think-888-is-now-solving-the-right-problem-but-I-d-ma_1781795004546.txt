I think #888 is now solving the right problem, but I'd make one major change before you build it:

Don't remove the payment expense entirely yet.

You are in the middle of redesigning:

Job costing
Sub payments
Sub portal
1099 reporting
Outstanding balances
Closed jobs
Year-end reports

That's a lot of accounting logic changing at once.

Instead I'd split this into Phase 1 and Phase 2.

Phase 1 (Build Now)

Create:

sub_payment_records

Update:

pay-worker

to write BOTH:

sub_payment_records

AND

expenses

for a short transition period.

Then:

Move portal payment history to sub_payment_records
Move 1099 calculations to sub_payment_records
Build payment history pages from sub_payment_records
Verify reports for a few weeks

Nothing breaks.

Phase 2 (After Validation)

Once you verify:

Portal works
1099 totals match
Payment history matches
Outstanding balances match

Then:

Remove expense creation from pay-worker

and clean up the old records.

The other thing I would add is:

Link payment records to bank transactions

Add nullable field:

bank_transaction_id INTEGER

to:

sub_payment_records

Not because you need it today.

Because later you'll want:

I paid James $417.50
Show me the bank transaction

or

This ACH payment cleared the bank
Match it to the sub payment

That becomes incredibly useful once Banking and Accounting are cleaned up.

One more field I'd add
remaining_balance NUMERIC(10,2)

No.

Do NOT store it.

Compute it.

Store:

amountOwed
amountPaid

Compute:

remainingBalance = amountOwed - amountPaid

everywhere.

Otherwise you'll eventually have:

Amount Owed: 617.50
Amount Paid: 417.50
Remaining: 250.00

because one field got updated and another didn't.

So if I were prioritizing:

Must Have

✅ sub_payment_records

✅ idempotency_key

✅ portal reads payment records

✅ 1099 reads payment records

Nice To Have

✅ bank_transaction_id

Skip

❌ storing remaining balance

And before building #888, I'd still finish #887 first.

Right now the Pay Breakdown screen is showing the wrong economics of the job. That's something you'll look at every day.

The payment-ledger redesign is important, but the waterfall math is the thing that affects every job immediately.
