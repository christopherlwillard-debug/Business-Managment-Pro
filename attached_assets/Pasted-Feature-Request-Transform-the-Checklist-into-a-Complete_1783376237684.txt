Feature Request: Transform the Checklist into a Complete Job Timeline

Overview

The current checklist works well for marking tasks complete, but it does not tell the complete story of the work performed. Once a comment is edited, previous information is mixed together or lost. Photos are attached to checklist items but do not show the progression of the work throughout the day.

I want to evolve the checklist into a living project timeline that automatically documents everything from the moment the crew arrives until the job is complete. The checklist should become a project history, not just a list of completed items.

Running Comment History

Currently each checklist item only supports one comment. If I need to update the comment later, I have to edit the original note. This causes multiple updates to become one long paragraph, making it difficult to understand what happened and when.

Instead, every new comment should create a new entry in a running history. Previous comments should never be overwritten.

Example:

9:12 AM - Chris Willard
Waiting on water crew to finish running water lines.

11:47 AM - Chris Willard
Water crew finished.

12:05 PM - Chris Willard
Started pressure testing.

2:18 PM - Chris Willard
Pressure test passed with no leaks.

3:40 PM - Chris Willard
Crew complete.

This preserves the entire history of the job and makes communication much clearer.

Automatic Timestamps

Every action should automatically record the date, time, and user who performed it.

Instead of only showing “July 6, 2026,” the checklist should show exactly when each event occurred.

For example:

July 6, 2026

9:14 AM - Drain connected

11:36 AM - Sewer connected

1:02 PM - Proper drain slope verified

2:18 PM - Pressure test completed

3:47 PM - Final inspection completed

This would provide much better documentation for warranty work, customer questions, and internal records.

Photo Progress Timeline

Photos should not simply appear underneath a completed checklist item. Every photo upload should become its own event in the timeline.

Example:

10:15 AM
Drain line installed
3 photos

11:42 AM
Final 90 installed
2 photos

2:20 PM
Pressure test completed
4 photos

This creates a visual history of the project instead of just a collection of finished photos.

Unified Activity Feed

Every action inside a checklist should automatically create an activity entry.

Examples include:

Checklist completed

Comment added

Photo uploaded

Split assigned

Marked N/A

Checklist reopened

Checklist completed again

The activity feed should provide a complete audit trail without requiring additional work from the crew.

Progress Photo Categories

Instead of only having “Finished Work Photos,” allow separate categories such as:

Before Work

During Work

Finished Work

Warranty or Service Photos

This keeps project documentation organized and makes it easier to review later.

Daily Work Log

For jobs lasting multiple days, automatically organize activity by day.

For example:

Monday

8 photos

14 comments

12 checklist items completed

Tuesday

6 photos

10 comments

8 checklist items completed

Wednesday

Final cleanup

Inspection

Customer walkthrough

This makes longer jobs much easier to review.

Photo Captions

Allow comments to be attached directly to each uploaded photo.

Examples:

Installed final 90 into existing sewer.

Customer requested pipe moved six inches.

Pressure test passed with no leaks.

This turns photos into searchable documentation instead of just images.

Status Updates

Sometimes work pauses even though no checklist items have been completed.

Allow crews to add status updates such as:

Waiting on water crew

Waiting on electrician

Waiting on inspection

Rain delay

Homeowner not available

Material delivery delayed

These updates should appear within the project timeline.

Task Duration Tracking

Automatically record when work begins and when it is completed.

Example:

Drain Connection

Started at 9:05 AM

Completed at 10:42 AM

Duration: 1 hour 37 minutes

This information can later be used for labor analytics and estimating future jobs.

Crew Activity

Instead of simply showing who completed a checklist item, record every action performed by every crew member.

For example:

Chris completed checklist.

Dewayne uploaded six photos.

Mike added a comment.

Chris completed final inspection.

This provides accountability and a complete history of crew activity.

Comment Types

Allow comments to be categorized.

Suggested categories include:

Progress

Waiting

Issue

Information

Examples:

Waiting on water crew.

Broken cleanout discovered.

Pressure test passed.

This helps important updates stand out from normal comments.

Better Progress Display

Instead of simply displaying “6 of 8 completed,” provide a more detailed progress summary.

For example:

75% Complete

6 of 8 checklist items finished

2 checklist items remaining

Estimated time remaining: 20 minutes

This gives crews and office staff a better understanding of job progress.

Continue Work Without Losing History

If work needs to continue after a checklist item has already been completed, users should not edit the original
