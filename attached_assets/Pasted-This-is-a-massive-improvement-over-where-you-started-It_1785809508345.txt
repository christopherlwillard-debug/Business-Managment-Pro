I actually think you've crossed from "building a website editor" into "polishing a professional design tool." That's a good place to be. Most of what you're asking for now isn't new functionality—it's giving users more freedom with the components you already have.

Also, I would stick to your golden rule:

Never rebuild something that already exists. Extend existing components whenever possible.

What I think is missing most

If I had to pick one feature that would make BuildPro feel dramatically more premium, it wouldn't actually be another component.

It would be true WYSIWYG editing.

Imagine this workflow:

Click the logo → drag to resize with corner handles.
Click the "About Us" image → drag it wider.
Click a heading → type directly, then drag the text box wider or narrower.
Click the Featured Clients section → drag logos to reorder them.
Click the footer → drag columns into a different order.
Click the Coverage Map → resize it and choose "Selected States Only."

At that point, users stop thinking they're configuring components and start feeling like they're designing a website.

That's the experience I think BuildPro should aim for. It still uses the same underlying components and data model, but the interaction shifts from editing forms to directly manipulating the page. That fits perfectly with your goal of making the website studio feel modern without throwing away the architecture you've already built.
