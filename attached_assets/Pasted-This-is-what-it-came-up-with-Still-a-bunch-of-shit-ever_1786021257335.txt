I think BuildPro should lean into being a single-screen management app where you can scroll one page and see everything. That's a real advantage over apps that bury everything behind tabs and menus.

The key isn't showing less—it's making sure every piece of information appears only once. If a number or status is already visible somewhere, don't show it again unless it's adding new context. That approach will make the app feel much cleaner while keeping everything accessible at a glance.
