Task #1416 – Dashboard & Accounting Organization Cleanup

Objective

This task is an organization and usability improvement only.

Do NOT redesign the application.
Do NOT add new APIs.
Do NOT change any calculations.
Do NOT change business logic.
Do NOT remove any existing functionality.

The goal is simply to eliminate duplicate information, group related information together, and make the Dashboard easier to understand at a glance.

------------------------------------------------------------

Dashboard Layout

Reorganize the Dashboard into the following sections in this exact order:

1. Today's Alerts
2. Business Snapshot
3. Profit
4. Subcontractor Payments
5. My Pay
6. Revenue vs Expenses

This should create a natural top-to-bottom workflow for running the business.

------------------------------------------------------------

Section 1 – Today's Alerts

Leave this section at the top.

Include existing cards only:

• Compliance
• Overdue Invoices
• Active Jobs
• Any existing alert cards

No logic changes.

------------------------------------------------------------

Section 2 – Business Snapshot

Group all existing business overview metrics together.

Examples:

• Revenue
• Expenses
• Active Jobs
• Overdue Invoices
• Collections

Do not add new metrics.

This section should simply answer:

"How is my business doing today?"

------------------------------------------------------------

Section 3 – Profit

Replace the two separate profit cards with one combined Profit card.

Current:

• Net Profit – Operational (YTD)
• Your Actual Profit (YTD)

Replace with:

Profit

Operational Profit
$X,XXX.XX

Less Outstanding Subcontractor Payments
-$X,XXX.XX

────────────────────────

Actual Profit
$X,XXX.XX

Small helper text:

"Actual Profit represents Operational Profit after all unpaid subcontractor obligations have been paid."

Do not change any calculations.

Only combine the existing information into one easier-to-understand card.

------------------------------------------------------------

Section 4 – Subcontractor Payments

Place this section directly below Profit.

Keep the existing information.

Group together:

• Total Paid
• Total Owed
• Remaining Balance

Include a "View Details" button or link if one already exists.

Reason:

The workflow should naturally be:

Business earns profit

↓

Pay subcontractors

↓

Then pay the owner

------------------------------------------------------------

Section 5 – My Pay

Keep My Pay on the Dashboard because it is checked frequently.

However, simplify it.

Keep:

• Earned
• Taken
• Owed
• Record Owner Pay button

Remove:

• Recent Owner Pay transaction list

Instead add:

View Full History →

This link should navigate directly to the Accounting Owner Pay page.

The Dashboard should only show the summary.

Accounting should contain the complete history.

------------------------------------------------------------

Section 6 – Revenue vs Expenses

Leave the existing chart below My Pay.

No changes to chart logic.

No API changes.

No redesign required.

------------------------------------------------------------

Accounting

Leave all detailed financial management inside Accounting.

Keep:

• Owner Pay History
• Owner Draw History
• Edit/Delete Owner Pay
• Business Reserve
• Owner Equity
• Banking
• Expenses
• Profit & Loss
• Revenue by City
• Cost Breakdown
• Other Income
• Reports
• Tax Reports

Accounting is the detailed financial workspace.

Dashboard is the quick operational overview.

------------------------------------------------------------

Remove Duplication

Currently the Dashboard and Accounting both display Owner Pay history.

Remove the recent transaction list from the Dashboard.

Keep the complete history only in Accounting.

The Dashboard should remain a quick summary.

------------------------------------------------------------

Visual Organization

Separate each section with consistent spacing and a section heading.

The page should read naturally:

Today's Alerts

↓

Business Snapshot

↓

Profit

↓

Subcontractor Payments

↓

My Pay

↓

Revenue vs Expenses

------------------------------------------------------------

Success Criteria

• No duplicate Owner Pay history on the Dashboard.
• Operational Profit and Actual Profit are combined into one Profit card.
• Subcontractor payment information is grouped into one section.
• My Pay remains on the Dashboard but is simplified.
• Dashboard tells the story of the business in the correct order:
  - What needs attention?
  - How is the business performing?
  - How much profit has been earned?
  - What still needs to be paid to subcontractors?
  - What can I pay myself?
  - How is the business trending?
• No APIs, calculations, or business logic are changed.
• This is an organization and usability improvement only.
