Overall Product Feedback

I think you're approaching a really important transition.

Version 1 thinking

"Every section needs settings."

↓

Version 2 thinking

"Every object should be editable."

↓

Version 3 thinking (where I think BuildPro should go)

"The inspector should almost disappear."

The canvas becomes the app.

If this were my product, my roadmap from here would be:
Phase 1 – Polish (Highest Priority)
Fix every broken interaction (Add Logo, image uploads, click targets).
Make every visible object directly editable.
Redesign the footer into block-based columns.
Reduce reliance on the right inspector.
Phase 2 – Professional Editing
Dockable/collapsible inspector.
Figma-style layer hierarchy in the left panel.
Drag-to-adjust padding and spacing.
Hover edit controls on every editable element.
Contextual toolbars instead of deep accordion menus.
Phase 3 – Premium Features
Section templates with live previews.
Global design system (colors, fonts, buttons, spacing).
Reusable components (save a section and reuse it across sites).
Full responsive editing with desktop/tablet/mobile overrides.

The biggest opportunity isn't adding dozens more sections—it's making the editing experience feel effortless. If someone can sit down with BuildPro and intuitively edit almost everything by clicking directly on the page, you'll have a much stronger product than one that simply has a longer list of components.
