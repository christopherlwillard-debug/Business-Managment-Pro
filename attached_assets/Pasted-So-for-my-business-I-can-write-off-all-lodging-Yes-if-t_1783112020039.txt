Yes—if the lodging is primarily for a legitimate business trip, you can generally deduct 100% of your lodging costs as a business expense.

For your situation, if you're traveling from Owensboro to Quincy, Indiana to perform work for a client, then expenses such as:

Hotel or motel
Airbnb or other short-term rental
Parking at the hotel
Hotel taxes and required fees

are generally deductible as ordinary and necessary business expenses.

You can also typically deduct:

Mileage (or actual vehicle expenses if that's the method you use)
Tolls and parking
Meals while traveling (subject to the current IRS rules on meal deductions)
Laundry/dry cleaning if you're on an extended business trip
Internet or business-related phone expenses while traveling

A few important limitations:

If you bring your family and they aren't traveling for a bona fide business purpose, their travel and lodging costs aren't deductible.
If you extend the trip for a personal vacation, only the business-related portion of the lodging is deductible.
