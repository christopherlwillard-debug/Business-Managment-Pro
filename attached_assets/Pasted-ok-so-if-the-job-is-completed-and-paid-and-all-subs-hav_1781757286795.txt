✅ Completed

Why This Matters

When you have 100+ jobs in the system, you won't want to scroll through giant cards for work that finished six months ago.

The cards should be your workbench.

The table should be your history book.

For Willard's GC, I'd tell Replit:

When a job is Completed, Customer Paid, and all subcontractor balances are settled, automatically move it to a new Closed status. Closed jobs should no longer render as full job cards. What I'd Tell Replit

Do not remove the job card data model. Closed jobs still contain photos, materials, notes, invoices, daily logs, and financial history. Instead, create a new Closed Jobs archive view. By default, closed jobs should appear in a compact table sorted newest-to-oldest. Each row can be expanded or opened to view the full archived job card with all associated photos, notes, materials, invoices, and logs. The goal is to reduce scrolling while preserving access to all historical job information.

That gives you the best of both worlds:

Active jobs = card workflow
Closed jobs = searchable archive
Nothing gets lost
Newest completed jobs always appear at the top instead of buried at the bottom of the page.
