BuildPro Feature Request – Create Job Expense from Material List

Objective

Add the ability to create a real Job Expense directly from an individual material line while building the Material List. This eliminates duplicate data entry and ensures accurate job costing.

Material Line

Each material line should include a checkbox:

☐ Add Cost to Job Expenses

This checkbox is per material, not for the entire material list.

⸻

Behavior

If unchecked

* Material is added to the invoice.
* Customer is billed normally.
* No Job Expense is created.
* Used for materials already owned by the business (existing stock, trailer inventory, leftover materials, etc.).

If checked

When the material is saved:

* Automatically create a Job Expense.
* Expense Category = Materials.
* Expense Amount = Material Cost (before markup).
* Description = Material Description.
* Link the expense to the originating material line.
* Include the expense in Job Profit calculations.
* Include the expense in Accounting & Tax reports.
* Display the expense on the Job Card under the Expenses section.

⸻

Example

Material List

Material	Qty	Cost	Sell Price	Add Cost to Job Expenses
90° PVC Elbow	2	$1.92	$7.68	☐
Toilet	1	$180.00	$275.00	☑

⸻

Invoice

90° PVC Elbow     $15.36
Toilet           $275.00

⸻

Job Card → Expenses

Materials
Toilet
$180.00
Source:
Auto-created from Material List

The PVC elbows do not appear in Expenses because they were already owned by the business and did not require a new purchase.

⸻

Synchronization Rules

* Editing the material quantity or cost automatically updates the linked Job Expense.
* Deleting the material automatically removes the linked Job Expense if it has not been manually modified.
* Manual Job Expenses continue to function exactly as they do today.

⸻

Goal

This feature allows contractors to decide material by material whether that item should become a real business expense.

Examples:

* Purchased specifically for the job → Check Add Cost to Job Expenses.
* Already had the material on the truck or shelf → Leave unchecked.
* Customer is billed for both materials regardless.

This keeps invoicing simple while ensuring the Job Card, Accounting, and Profit calculations reflect only the actual costs incurred by the business.
