#1080 - Job travel expenses as documentation only
What & Why
The owner travels for jobs (e.g. Owensboro → Quincy, IN) and needs lodging/meals/travel costs tied to the job as supporting documentation — an audit trail proving why the expense occurred (receipt + dates + job address + business purpose in one place) — WITHOUT those costs reducing the job's profit figure. Today any job-linked expense is subtracted from job profit, and the existing "exclude from net profit" toggle removes the deduction entirely (wrong for travel — it IS deductible). The design separates two questions: "how profitable was this job?" (job costing) vs "what did the business spend that's deductible?" (tax records). Travel costs should hit the business P&L (fully deductible) but be separable from job costing.

Done looks like
The expense form (add + edit) offers "Travel/Lodging" and "Meals" categories; the P&L report shows them on the proper Schedule C lines (Travel 24a full; Meals 24b at 50%)
When an expense is linked to a job, a new toggle appears: "Count toward job profit?" — on by default (job cost), off = the expense is linked as supporting documentation only (with a short helper line explaining doc-only still counts as a business write-off)
Choosing "Travel/Lodging" or "Meals" with a job selected defaults the toggle to documentation-only (user can override)
The existing notes field is surfaced as "Business purpose" on the expense forms (placeholder examples like "Hotel for Clayton service work in Quincy, IN"), giving each expense audit-trail context; shown on doc-only rows and in the job travel summary
The Banking page's create-expense flow (from a bank transaction) offers the same new categories, the same toggle, and the business-purpose field, so a Hampton Inn or restaurant transaction can be linked to a job as documentation in one step
Doc-only expenses no longer reduce the job's profit; the job financials panel shows two groups: "Job Costs" (counted) and a Travel Summary block — line items with category, vendor, business purpose, and amount, a total, and two check notes: "Not included in job profit" and "Included in business P&L"
The expense list shows a small badge on doc-only rows
Business-wide P&L / dashboard totals still include these expenses exactly as before (fully deductible; meals at 50% on the P&L report)
A simple travel summary is visible in reports: travel + meals totals for the year, with a per-job breakdown of documentation-only expenses (answers "this out-of-town job required $300 of travel")
Existing job-linked expenses are unaffected (flag defaults to counted)
Out of scope
Changing the existing "exclude from net profit" (Owner Draw style) behavior
Mileage handling (already has its own IRS-rate deduction flow)
Auto-categorization of bank transactions by vendor name (future idea)
A dedicated business-purpose column (reuse the existing notes column; relabel in the UI)
Applying the flag or new categories to production data (dev only; prod column arrives via the next Publish per project convention — never add a DDL migration endpoint)
Steps
Schema + API — Add a boolean countsTowardJobProfit (default true — positive flag per review, avoid a negative name) to the expenses table; accept it in the expense create/update routes and return it in expense payloads. Apply the column to the dev database.
Job profit calculation — Only sum expenses where the flag is true into job expenses in the job profit endpoint; return doc-only expenses as a separate documentation list/total so the UI can display both groups.
Expense form UI — Add the "Travel/Lodging" and "Meals" categories; show the "Count toward job profit?" toggle when a job is selected (add and edit flows) with smart defaults by category; relabel notes as "Business purpose" with audit-trail placeholder examples; add a badge on doc-only rows in the expense list.
Banking page flow — Add the same categories, toggle, and business-purpose labeling to the create-expense form used when converting a bank transaction, including the category-based default.
Job financials panel — Show "Job Costs" and a "Travel Summary" block as separate groups on the job card financials; the summary lists each doc-only expense (category, vendor, purpose, amount) with a total and "Not included in job profit / Included in business P&L" notes.
Reports — Verify the P&L maps the two new categories to Travel (24a, 100%) and Meals (24b, 50%) and that doc-only expenses still flow into business P&L totals; add a travel summary with per-job documentation-expense totals to the reports page.
Verify — API tests for the profit calc (doc-only excluded from job profit, included in P&L) and a manual pass on the expense form, Banking flow, and job card.
