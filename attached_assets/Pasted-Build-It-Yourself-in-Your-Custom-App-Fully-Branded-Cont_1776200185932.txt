Build It Yourself in Your Custom App (Fully Branded & Controlled)
If you want it native to your app (white-labeled experience, custom pricing engine, customer portal, etc.), here’s exactly how to do it:
1.  Satellite View + Address Lookup
	•  Use Google Maps JavaScript API (web), Maps SDK for Android/iOS, or cross-platform (Flutter/React Native Google Maps plugin / Mapbox).
	•  User enters address or drops a pin → switch to satellite or hybrid layer (shows pavement clearly).
	•  Optional: Add Street View for extra verification.
2.  Draw & Measure Asphalt Area
	•  Let users tap points to draw a polygon around the driveway/lot (or multiple polygons).
	•  Use Google’s Geometry Library: google.maps.geometry.spherical.computeArea(polygon.getPath()) → converts to sq ft (×10.764 from sq meters).
	•  Or with Mapbox/Turf.js: turf.area(polygon) (super accurate and free/open-source).
	•  Bonus: Also calculate perimeter (for edging or crack sealing linear ft) with the same libraries.
3.  Build the Quoting Engine Once you have the area (e.g., 1,250 sq ft):
	•  Base price = area × your rate per sq ft (make this editable per job/region).
	•  Materials: Sealer gallons = (area ÷ coverage per gallon) × coats (common: 80–150 sq ft/gallon per coat; 2 coats typical). Add sand/additives if needed.
	•  Labor: Hours = area ÷ your sq ft per hour rate + setup/travel.
	•  Add-ons: Crack sealing (linear ft × rate), patching, striping, etc.
	•  Total quote + tax, discounts, notes.
	•  Show a preview with the satellite map overlay + outlined area for professionalism.
4.  Extra Polish for Asphalt Sealing Apps
	•  Save the polygon coordinates + map screenshot to the job record.
	•  Generate PDF quote with map image, breakdown, and “Measurements are estimates—final verified on-site” disclaimer (satellite can be off by 5–10% due to angles/trees).
	•  Customer self-service portal: They outline their own driveway and get an instant quote request.
	•  Offline mode (cache tiles) or GPS walk-perimeter for on-site accuracy.
	•  AI upgrade (optional): Use computer vision (e.g., via Google Vision or custom model) to suggest asphalt outlines automatically.
Tech Stack Suggestion (cross-platform easiest)
•  Flutter or React Native → one codebase for iOS/Android/web.
•  Google Maps or Mapbox for imagery (Mapbox often cheaper for heavy use).
•  Backend (Firebase/Supabase/Node) for storing jobs, customers, and pricing rules.
•  PDF generation (pdf-lib or similar).
Tutorials for the mapping part are everywhere (search “Google Maps polygon area calculator Flutter” or “Mapbox Turf.js area measurement”). The quoting math is just basic formulas you control.
Quick Start Tip
If you want to prototype right now (no coding): Try the free Action Seal Surface Area Measurement Tool or Google Earth Pro—they use the exact same satellite tech and have built-in calculators for sealer/crack filler. Then replicate that flow in your app.
