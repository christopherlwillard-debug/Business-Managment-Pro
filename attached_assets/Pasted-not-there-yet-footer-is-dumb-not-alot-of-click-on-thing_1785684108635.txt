Editor Improvements

These would make editing feel much more modern:

Right-click menu on every component (Edit, Duplicate, Delete, Hide, Save as Template).
Global Styles panel to update fonts, colors, button styles, border radius, and spacing site-wide.
Navigator breadcrumbs (Page → Section → Card → Button) so users always know where they are.
Undo history panel instead of only step-by-step undo.
Responsive editing with separate desktop, tablet, and mobile spacing controls.
My biggest recommendation

The builder should feel like Canva meets Framer, not like a settings page.

Almost everything should be editable by clicking directly on it.

When I see a phone number, I should click the phone number.

When I see a logo, I should click the logo.

When I see text, I should click the text.

When I see an icon, I should click the icon.

When I see whitespace, I should click it to adjust spacing.

The right sidebar should become a property inspector for whatever is selected—not the primary way to discover what can be edited.

From these screenshots, I'd honestly say you're about 75–80% of the way there. The underlying structure looks solid. The remaining work is less about adding lots of new features and more about making the editing experience feel obvious, direct, and effortless. That polish is what will make people compare it favorably to premium builders instead of feeling like they're filling out forms.
