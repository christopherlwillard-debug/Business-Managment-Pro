#1321 - Unified Banking Reconciliation Engine

## What & Why

The Banking page should not be an expense management page. It should be the central reconciliation hub for the entire accounting system.

Every downloaded bank transaction represents one financial event. The system's job is to automatically determine what that event represents and link it to the correct accounting record.

Rather than only matching transactions to expenses, the reconciliation engine should attempt to match every transaction against all supported accounting record types.

This creates a single intelligent workflow that scales as new accounting features are added.

The objective is that 90-95% of downloaded bank transactions require zero user interaction.

---

## Design Philosophy

Expenses remain the accounting ledger.

The Banking page becomes an inbox that verifies and reconciles financial activity.

Instead of asking:

"What expense does this belong to?"

The system asks:

"What accounting event does this transaction represent?"

---

## Reconciliation Flow

Every new bank transaction follows the same process.

New Bank Transaction

↓

Attempt Automatic Reconciliation

↓

Search supported accounting records

↓

Highest confidence match found?

YES

↓

Automatically Link

↓

Mark Reconciled

NO

↓

Needs Attention

---

## Reconciliation Priority

The engine should search in this order.

1. Existing Expense
    • AI receipt scanned
    • Manual expense
    • Imported expense

2. Subcontractor Payment
    • Outstanding subcontractor payment
    • Payroll payment
    • Advance repayment

3. Investment Transfer
    • Brokerage transfer
    • Investment contribution

4. Customer Payment (Income)
    • Invoice payment
    • Customer deposit

5. Internal Bank Transfer
    • Checking to Savings
    • Savings to Checking
    • Business account transfers

6. Owner Activity
    • Owner Draw
    • Owner Contribution

7. Personal Transaction

8. Unknown

The first high-confidence match wins.

If no match exceeds the confidence threshold, the transaction moves to Needs Attention.

## Future Extensibility

The reconciliation engine should be designed so additional accounting record types can easily be added later.

Future examples include:

• Credit Card Payments

• Loan Payments

• Tax Payments

• Equipment Financing

• Vehicle Loans

• Asset Purchases

• Depreciation Assets

• Insurance Payments

Each new feature simply registers another reconciliation provider without changing the Banking page workflow.

---

## Benefits

• One intelligent reconciliation engine for the entire accounting system.

• Eliminates separate matching logic for expenses, payroll, investments, transfers, and income.

• Greatly reduces manual bookkeeping.

• Allows AI receipt scanning, payroll, invoicing, investments, and banking to work together seamlessly.

• Creates a Banking page that serves as a true accounting inbox instead of a manual matching screen.

• Scales naturally as additional accounting features are added in the future.

• Makes reconciliation simple: the system automatically identifies what each transaction represents and only asks the user for help when confidence is too low.
