# Business Reserve Dashboard (Master Reserve Card)

## Goal

Create a new "Business Reserve Dashboard" card at the top of the Accounting page that combines all required business reserves into one place. Instead of making the owner mentally add multiple sections together, the app should answer one simple question:

"How much money in my business checking account is already spoken for?"

This should become the primary cash planning dashboard for the business.

---

## Main Card

Title:
Business Reserve Dashboard

Large Display:

KEEP IN BUSINESS CHECKING

$X,XXX.XX

Subtitle:

Minimum amount that should remain in your business checking account today.

---

## Reserve Breakdown

The required reserve is calculated from four separate sections that already exist in the app.

1. Tax Reserve
- Uses the existing Tax Reserve value.
- This is actual cash intentionally set aside.
- This is NOT the Estimated Tax Liability.

2. Growth Fund
- Uses the available Growth Fund balance.
- (Accumulated - Invested)

3. Business Overhead Reserve
- Uses active recurring expenses.
- Display next 30 days of recurring business expenses.
- Future enhancement: Allow owner to keep 1, 2, or 3 months of overhead.

4. Optional Operating Reserve
- User configurable.
- Example:
  - Keep 1 month of overhead
  - Keep 2 months
  - Keep 3 months
  - Custom amount

---

## Formula

Required Business Reserve =

Tax Reserve

+ Available Growth Fund

+ Next 30 Days Recurring Expenses

+ Operating Reserve (if enabled)

=

Minimum Checking Balance

Calculate:

Examples:

115 Days

or

3.8 Months

Formula:

Business Reserve Available for Overhead

÷

Monthly Recurring Expenses

This gives the owner an immediate understanding of how long the business could continue operating without new income.

---

## Separate Tax Estimate from Tax Reserve

These should remain completely separate.

Estimated Tax Liability

Purpose:
Accounting estimate based on current taxable profit.

Example:

Estimated Taxes Owed

$208.36

Tax Reserve

Purpose:
Actual cash intentionally reserved for taxes.

Example:

Tax Reserve

$2,320.29

The dashboard should clearly communicate:

Estimated Tax Liability = What you are expected to owe.

Tax Reserve = Cash you have already protected.

These are different numbers and should never be combined.

---

## Existing Data

This feature should reuse existing calculations already present in the app.

Use:

- Estimated Tax Liability
- Tax Reserve
- Growth Fund
- Recurring Expenses
- Connected Business Bank Balance (Plaid)
- Investment Tracking
- Business Settings

No duplicate calculations should be created.

---

## Primary Goal

The Business Reserve Dashboard should provide one trustworthy number that answers:

"How much money must remain in my business checking account today?"

Everything above that amount is safe to use for:
- Owner draws
- Equipment purchases
- Investments
- Expansion
- Other discretionary spending

This creates a true cash planning dashboard rather than simply displaying accounting data.
