Suggested Improvement

Instead of treating lunch as just a deduction, model it internally as an Advance Against Wages.

This keeps the accounting accurate because the subcontractor has already received value earlier in the day. It also makes the feature much more flexible for future use.

Rename the deduction fields throughout the database and API to:

* Advance Amount
* Advance Note

The UI can still use examples such as:

* Lunch advance
* Fuel advance
* Tool advance
* Cash advance

This allows any type of advance to be recorded without changing the workflow.

⸻

Store the Complete Payment Breakdown

Do not store only the final payment amount. Persist every component of the payment so the ledger can always be reconstructed later.

Store:

* Base Pay
* Work Hours
* Work Rate
* Travel Hours
* Travel Rate
* Bonus Amount
* Bonus Note
* Advance Amount
* Advance Note
* Net Paid

Calculate:

Base Pay = (Work Hours × Work Rate) + (Travel Hours × Travel Rate)

Net Paid = Base Pay + Bonus Amount − Advance Amount

Keeping every component allows future reporting, audits, payment history, and potential 1099 reporting without losing any information.

⸻

Improve the Payment History

Instead of only displaying the net payment amount, show the complete breakdown for every payment.

Example:

Paid: $455.00

Work:
12.0 hrs × $25.00 = $300.00

Travel:
8.0 hrs × $15.00 = $120.00

Performance Bonus:
+$50.00
“Finished punch list ahead of schedule”

Lunch Advance:
−$15.00

Net Paid:
$455.00

This makes the ledger self-documenting so months later it is immediately obvious how every payment was calculated.

⸻

Future-Proofing

Use the term Advance instead of Deduction throughout the database schema, API, and business logic.

An advance simply reduces the cash paid because the subcontractor has already received part of their compensation earlier (lunch, fuel, tools, cash, etc.).

This leaves room in the future to add true payroll deductions (taxes, insurance, garnishments, retirement contributions, etc.) without renaming database fields or performing migrations.

This change makes the system more accurate from an accounting perspective while also making it more flexible for future payroll and reporting features.
