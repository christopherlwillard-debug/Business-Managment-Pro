The bug is that "Record Owner Pay" is being saved as a Business Expense (Owner Compensation), causing it to flow back into Job Expenses and reduce future profit calculations.

Instead:

1. Record Owner Pay as an Owner Distribution / Owner Draw transaction.
2. Exclude Owner Distribution records from all expense totals and P&L calculations.
3. Keep a separate Owner Activity ledger for reporting all owner draws.
4. Do not allow Owner Draws to affect:
   - Business Expenses
   - Remaining Profit
   - Split Pool
   - Growth Reserve
   - Job Profit
5. Owner Draws should only reduce the remaining amount available to withdraw from that job and appear in the Owner Activity history.

The Pay Breakdown page is a cash allocation workflow, not a Profit & Loss statement. Preserve the workflow exactly as designed while correcting the accounting classification of owner pay.

I actually like your workflow. It's easy to understand because it answers the question you care about after every job:

That's a much more practical view for an owner-operator than a traditional accounting report—as long as the underlying owner draw is classified correctly.
