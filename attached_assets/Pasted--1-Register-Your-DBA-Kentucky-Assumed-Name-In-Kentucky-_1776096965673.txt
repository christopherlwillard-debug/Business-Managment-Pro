✅ 1. Register Your DBA (Kentucky “Assumed Name”)
In Kentucky, a DBA is called an “Assumed Name.”
Where to file:
Kentucky Secretary of State


What to do:
File a Certificate of Assumed Name for something like:


Willard’s Asphalt Sealing


Willard’s Sealcoating & Pavement Maintenance


Cost:
~$20–$30 (one-time)


Important:
You’re not creating a new business — it’s still Willard’s General Contracting LLC, just operating under another name.



✅ 2. Also File at the County Level (Recommended)
Even though the state filing is the main one, it’s smart to also file with:
Daviess County Clerk’s Office


This helps locally (banks, permits, contracts, etc.).
Cost:
Usually ~$20–$50



✅ 3. Update Your Owensboro Business License
Make sure your DBA shows up locally too via:
Owensboro Occupational License Division


What to say:
“I operate as Willard’s General Contracting LLC and I’m adding a DBA: Willard’s Asphalt Sealing for pavement maintenance services.”
This keeps everything clean if a customer or inspector ever looks you up.

✅ 4. Use the DBA Properly (This Part Matters)
Once filed, you can legally market and operate as:
Willard’s Asphalt Sealing (a DBA of Willard’s General Contracting LLC)


Use it on:
Yard signs


Estimates & invoices


Facebook / Google Business


Shirts, trailer branding


But legally:
Contracts should still say:

 Willard’s General Contracting LLC, DBA Willard’s Asphalt Sealing


That protects you if anything ever goes sideways.

✅ 5. Banking & Payments (Optional but Powerful)
You’ve got two solid options:
Option A (Simple — recommended to start)
Keep using your current LLC bank account

Track sealcoating separately in your books


Option B (More “pro” look later)
Open a separate DBA checking account


Bank will ask for:

This makes you look bigger + keeps revenue streams clean.

✅ 6. Insurance — Tie the DBA In

This is key because:
Customers hiring “Willard’s Asphalt Sealing” need to see that name on the insurance

1. SIMPLE PARKING LOT BID CALCULATOR (USE THIS EVERY TIME)
You only need 4 inputs:
Step 1: Measure square footage
Length × width (or multiple sections added together)



💰 Pricing Formula (keep it simple)
Base pricing:
Small lots (<10,000 sq ft):

 👉 $0.25 – $0.35 / sq ft


Medium (10,000–25,000 sq ft):

 👉 $0.20 – $0.30 / sq ft


Large (25,000+ sq ft):

 👉 $0.18 – $0.25 / sq ft



🧱 Cost estimate (your system)
Sealer cost: $0.07–$0.09 / sq ft


Misc (fuel, tape, wear): $0.02 / sq ft


👉 Total cost: ~$0.10–$0.11 / sq ft

💵 Profit formula
Profit per sq ft = Your price – $0.10
Example:
Charge $0.25


Cost $0.10

 👉 Profit = $0.15/sq ft



📊 Quick Example
10,000 sq ft lot:
Charge: $0.25 → $2,500


Cost: $1,000

 👉 Profit: $1,500
