About Us / Company Introduction
At Willard’s General Contracting, we specialize in completing the final stages of manufactured home installation once your home is delivered and set on site. We handle the critical finish work that transforms a manufactured unit into a polished, comfortable, move-in-ready home.
From seamless interior marriage lines and exterior siding to final plumbing connections and thorough inspections, we focus on the details that matter most. We treat every project as if it were our own — with honesty, care, and a commitment to quality that lasts for years.

Our Services
Manufactured Home Finish & Set Completion
Once your manufactured home is delivered and placed, we take over the final stages to make it fully complete and ready for you to move in. Our experienced team handles all the important details so you don’t have to.
Marriage Line Interior Finishing
We professionally tape, mud, and finish interior marriage lines for a seamless, invisible look. This includes drywall repairs, texture matching, trim installation, and precise paint touch-ups.
Exterior Marriage Line Siding
We install matching siding to cover and seal the exterior marriage line, providing a clean appearance and reliable weather protection.
Final Plumbing Connections
Our team completes all under-home plumbing tie-ins, including connecting drain lines to your septic or sewer system, installing cleanouts, and linking the main water supply.
Exterior Detailing & Inspection
We thoroughly inspect siding, trim, and all exterior finishes for any transport or setup damage, making repairs as needed for a flawless result.
Systems Check & Final Walkthrough
Before you take possession, we perform a detailed inspection of all fixtures, finishes, and systems to ensure everything meets quality standards.
Custom Projects & Site Improvements
We also handle select additional work such as building raised A/C platforms in flood-prone areas and other protective or functional improvements.
