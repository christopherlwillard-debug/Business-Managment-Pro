Invoice Workflow Redesign

The goal is to separate job completion from accounting while keeping invoice numbers perfectly sequential for audit compliance.

Invoices should not receive an invoice number when they are created. They should remain editable drafts attached only to the Job Card until they have been reviewed and approved.

Draft Stage

When a job is completed, the user can generate an invoice from the Job Card.

While the invoice is in Draft status:

* It exists only on the Job Card.
* It does not have an invoice number.
* It can be edited, regenerated, or deleted without affecting invoice numbering.
* It does not appear on the main Invoices page.
* It is considered a work-in-progress, not an official accounting document.

The Job Card should display the draft invoice with options to:

* Review Invoice
* Edit Invoice
* Print Preview
* PDF Preview
* Accept Invoice

Approve Invoice

The “Accept Invoice” button is the point where the invoice becomes official.

When the user clicks Accept Invoice, the system should:

* Assign the next sequential invoice number.
* Record the approval date and user.
* Lock the invoice snapshot for audit purposes (or create an audit trail for future edits).
* Move the invoice from the Job Card into the Invoices module.
* Remove the editable draft from the Job Card.
* Change the invoice status to Approved.

This is the first point where the invoice officially exists for accounting purposes.

Approved Status

Approved means the invoice has been reviewed, finalized, and is ready to be sent to the customer.

Approved invoices should appear on the Invoices page and be available for:

* Viewing
* Printing
* Downloading as PDF
* Emailing
* Texting
* Sharing
* Marking as Sent

If the user opens the send dialog but closes it without sending, the invoice should remain in Approved status. Simply opening the send window should never change the status.

Sent Status

An invoice should only move to Sent when delivery is actually confirmed.

This can happen when:

* An email is successfully sent.
* A text message is successfully sent.
* A share link is confirmed.
* The user manually clicks “Mark as Sent.”

Once sent, the system should record:

* Sent date and time.
* Delivery method.
* User who sent it.

Payment Progression

After being sent, invoices move through the normal payment workflow:

Approved → Sent → Partially Paid → Paid

If necessary, an Approved or Sent invoice can be marked Void, but it should never be permanently deleted once it has been assigned an invoice number.

Job Card Behavior

Before approval, the Job Card should show the editable draft invoice.

After approval, the draft should no longer appear. Instead, the Job Card should display a simple invoice summary with the assigned invoice number, current status, and a “View Invoice” button.

The Job Card should remain linked to the invoice for reference but should no longer allow editing after approval.

Benefits

This workflow separates production from accounting.

The Job Card remains the workspace for completing the job and building the invoice, while the Invoices page contains only finalized invoices that are part of the accounting system.

Invoice numbers are assigned only after approval, ensuring there are no gaps caused by abandoned or deleted drafts. Once an invoice receives a number, it becomes a permanent accounting record and can only progress through Approved, Sent, Partially Paid, Paid, or Void, maintaining complete audit compliance.
