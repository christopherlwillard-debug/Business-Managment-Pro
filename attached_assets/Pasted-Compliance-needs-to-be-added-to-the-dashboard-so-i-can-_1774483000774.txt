1. Annual Report with Kentucky Secretary of State (Required for Good Standing)
•  What it is: A simple update confirming or updating your LLC’s principal office address, registered agent/office, and member/manager info (for single-member LLCs, this is typically just you).
•  Due date: Anytime between January 1 and June 30 each year (your first one is due in the calendar year after formation). The state usually mails a postcard reminder in January.
•  Fee: $15 (paid with the report).
•  How to file: Online (preferred and fastest) at the Secretary of State’s FastTrack portal, or return the postcard by mail/in person.
•  Why it matters: This is the main filing that keeps your LLC in good standing. Missing the June 30 deadline puts you in bad standing; you get a 60-day notice, and failure to file by August 31 can lead to administrative dissolution (your LLC is dissolved and loses protections). Reinstatement is possible but involves extra steps and fees.
•  Official source: Kentucky Secretary of State (sos.ky.gov) or Kentucky Business One Stop (onestop.ky.gov).
2. Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET) – Form 725 (State Business Tax Filing)
•  What it is: Kentucky’s tax on limited-liability entities (including single-member LLCs, even if treated as a “disregarded entity” for income tax purposes). It’s based on Kentucky gross receipts or gross profits (minimum $175 if ≤ $3 million; higher sliding scale above that). It’s separate from your personal income tax but filed together in many cases.
•  Due date: April 15 (for calendar-year filers; 15th day of the 4th month after your tax year ends). Estimated payments required if you expect liability over $5,000.
•  How to file: Use Form 725 (Kentucky Single Member LLC Individually Owned Income and LLET Return). It can often be e-filed with your personal Kentucky individual return (Form 740) via tax software. Payment goes to the Kentucky Department of Revenue.
•  Electronic filing: Required if federal gross receipts > $1 million.
•  Note: This is the key state-level tax filing for your LLC (beyond personal income taxes). Late filing incurs penalties and interest.
3. Federal Tax Filings (IRS)
•  What it is: Your single-member LLC is a disregarded entity, so business income/loss flows to your personal taxes.
	•  File Form 1040 + Schedule C (profit/loss from business) and Schedule SE (self-employment tax if applicable).
	•  Make quarterly estimated tax payments (Form 1040-ES) if you expect to owe $1,000+ in taxes.
•  Due date: April 15 (or extension to October 15).
•  EIN: You should have one (easy to get from IRS.gov); it’s required for banking/taxes in most cases.
4. Sales & Use Tax (If Applicable)
•  If your LLC sells taxable goods, services, or collects sales tax, you need a sales tax permit (registered once via Kentucky Department of Revenue/Kentucky Online Gateway).
•  File periodic sales tax returns (monthly, quarterly, or annual depending on your volume).
•  No filing if you have zero taxable sales, but you must still be registered if required by your business type.
5. Local Occupational/Business License & Net Profit Fee (Owensboro & Daviess County – Required)
•  What it is: Owensboro/Daviess County requires an occupational business license for any person or entity doing business there. You pay a minimum annual fee plus a net profit license fee (greater of $75 or 1.78% of net profits from activity in Owensboro).
•  Due/renewal: Initial application before starting; then annual Net Profit License Fee Return (filed at the end of your tax year, along with payment). If you have employees, there’s also payroll withholding (filed quarterly/monthly).
•  Fee: $75 minimum annual license fee (City of Owensboro administers for both city and county).
•  How to handle: File the Occupational Business License Application with the Occupational Tax Administrator (City of Owensboro). Contact: (270) 687-5600 or check owensboro.org or daviessky.org.
•  Why it matters: Operating without it can lead to fines or shutdown.
6. Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) Report – Federal (FinCEN)
•  One-time initial report (filed online at FinCEN.gov/BOI) with your name, address, ID info, and beneficial owners (for single-member LLC: usually just you).
•  Not annual—only update within 30 days if ownership/control changes. No routine yearly filing unless something changes.
7. Other Recurring or Possible Requirements
•  Registered agent: Must keep a valid one in Kentucky (can be you or a service). Update via SOS if it changes.
•  Personal property tax: If your LLC owns business personal property (equipment, furniture, etc.), file an annual return with your county Property Valuation Administrator (usually due around May 15).
•  Employees (if any): Unemployment insurance, workers’ comp insurance, and withholding tax returns (quarterly/annual).
•  Industry-specific licenses/permits: Many professions (contractors, food service, etc.) require annual renewal—check Kentucky Department of Professional Licensing or onestop.ky.gov.
•  Record-keeping: Maintain internal financial records, operating agreement updates, etc. (not filed, but required for audits/taxes).

import csv
import os

def append_to_csv(file_path, data_dict, columns):
    # Check if the file exists; if not, we create it with headers
    file_exists = os.path.isfile(file_path)
    
    with open(file_path, mode='a', newline='') as f:
        writer = csv.DictWriter(f, fieldnames=columns)
        # If it's a brand new file, we write the headers first
        if not file_exists:
            writer.writeheader()
        writer.writerow(data_dict)

if not os.path.exists(RECEIPT_DIR):
    os.makedirs(RECEIPT_DIR)

def save_receipt(uploaded_file):
    """Saves the uploaded file and returns the local path."""
    if uploaded_file is not None:
        # Create a unique filename: receipt_20240325_1430.jpg
        timestamp = datetime.now().strftime("%Y%m%d_%H%M%S")
        file_ext = uploaded_file.name.split(".")[-1]
        file_name = f"receipt_{timestamp}.{file_ext}"
        file_path = os.path.join(RECEIPT_DIR, file_name)
        
        with open(file_path, "wb") as f:
            f.write(uploaded_file.getbuffer())
        return file_path
    return ""

                "AMOUNT": st.column_config.NumberColumn("Amount", format="$%.2f"),
                "DATE": st.column_config.DateColumn("Date")
            },
            hide_index=True,
            use_container_width=True,
            on_select="rerun",
            selection_mode="single_row"
        )

        # 3. THE PREVIEW POP-UP LOGIC
        # Check if a row was selected
        selected_rows = event.selection.rows
        if selected_rows:
            index = selected_rows[0]
            receipt_path = df_exp.iloc[index]["RECEIPT"]
            merchant_name = df_exp.iloc[index]["MERCHANT"]
            
            # Create a nice preview area
            st.divider()
            col1, col2 = st.columns([1, 2])
            
            with col1:
                st.write(f"### Receipt Preview")
                st.write(f"**Merchant:** {merchant_name}")
                st.write(f"**Date:** {df_exp.iloc[index]['DATE']}")
                st.write(f"**Amount:** ${df_exp.iloc[index]['AMOUNT']}")
            
            with col2:
                if pd.isna(receipt_path) or receipt_path == "":
                    st.warning("No receipt image attached to this record.")
                elif receipt_path.startswith("http"):
                    # It's an external link (like your S3 links)
                    st.image(receipt_path, caption=f"Receipt from {merchant_name}")
                elif os.path.exists(receipt_path):
                    # It's a local file in your Replit 'receipts/' folder
                    st.image(receipt_path, caption=f"Local Receipt: {receipt_path}")
                else:
                    st.error(f"File not found at: {receipt_path}")

    except Exception as e:
        st.error(f"Error loading records: {e}")

Why this is the "Pro" way to do it:
Contextual Viewing: You don't have to hunt for a file. If you see a weird $291.89 charge from Home Depot, you just click that row, and the photo appears instantly right there.
Hybrid Support: I wrote the code to handle both your old data (the https://s3.amazonaws... links) and your new data (the local receipts/image.jpg files). The app will automatically figure out which one it is and display it correctly.
No Extra Windows: It keeps everything inside one clean mobile screen. If you're on your phone, the image will appear neatly below the list.
What to check in Replit:
Make sure you have pip install pandas streamlit in your requirements.txt.
If you have a lot of receipts, Replit might start to run low on space. Since these are text and small images, you should be fine for a long time
