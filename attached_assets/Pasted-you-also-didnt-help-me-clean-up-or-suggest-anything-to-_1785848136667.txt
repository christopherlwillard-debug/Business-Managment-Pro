My priority list before Phase 5 AI

If I were prioritizing the Website Studio itself, I'd do it in this order:

Direct canvas editing (drag, resize, inline editing) so users spend less time in side panels.
Consistent image and logo controls across every component, with per-image sizing and normalization.
Modular header and footer builders where each block can be reordered, hidden, or duplicated.
Visual spacing and alignment tools with drag handles and snap guides.
Component swapping (change a Services section from cards to icons without rebuilding it).
Property consistency so every component exposes sizing, typography, spacing, and visibility in the same way.
Then add the AI Website Builder to automate those same actions.

That sequence follows your "don't rebuild what's already there" rule. You're not replacing your Website Studio—you'd be making the existing editor feel dramatically more powerful and intuitive by exposing the capabilities you already have in a much more visual way.
