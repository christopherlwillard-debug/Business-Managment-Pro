If this were my app

I'd actually change the wording in Task #1008.

Instead of:

Apply the linked benchmark.

I'd make it:

Apply the model defaults.

Where model defaults include:

Pricing
Suggested hours
Suggested crew
Checklist

Those defaults can originate from a benchmark if you want, but the job doesn't need to know that. It only needs the resulting values.

I think this is a cleaner long-term architecture because it separates two concepts:

Benchmarks = generic expectations for a job type (useful for reporting and planning).
Manufactured Home Library = specific knowledge about individual makes and models that drives your day-to-day work.

That matches the way you've described your business. You don't just want a benchmark—you want the app to learn everything about each manufactured home model over time.
