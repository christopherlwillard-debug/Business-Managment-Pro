I’d only make a couple of refinements.

#1607 – Receipt-First Expense Entry

Rating: 10/10

No changes. This is exactly how people use expense tracking in the field.

One small addition I’d consider:

After AI scans the receipt, show a confidence badge (“High Confidence”, “Medium Confidence”) and highlight any fields that need review before saving.

That builds trust in the AI.

⸻

#1608 – Income Breakdown

Rating: 9.8/10

I like everything except one thing.

Instead of “Breakdown by Job”, I’d rename it to:

Income by Job

or

Earnings by Job

Those are simpler and more natural for most subcontractors.

I’d also add one line to the “Done looks like” section:

The expanded state is remembered while the user remains in the portal session.

That way if they expand it once, it doesn’t keep collapsing every time they navigate around.

⸻

#1609 – Dashboard Overhaul

Rating: 10/10

This is exactly the direction I’d take.

One thing I’d tweak is the Tax Readiness checklist.

Instead of:

* Income Recorded
* Mileage Logged
* W-9 Completed
* Expenses Logged
* Home Office Set Up

I’d make it:

* ✅ W-9 Completed
* ✅ Income Recorded
* ✅ Expenses Logged
* ✅ Mileage Logged
* ✅ Tax Profile Complete (or Home Office Set Up if that’s already implemented)

The W-9 is the biggest blocker to getting a 1099 issued, so surfacing it first makes the checklist more logical.

⸻

One Feature I’d Add Next

After these three tasks, I’d create:

1610 – Smart Notifications & Action Center

Instead of users hunting for problems, the portal proactively tells them what needs attention.

Examples:

* Complete your W-9 to receive a 1099.
* You have 3 unpaid jobs awaiting payment.
* You haven’t logged mileage in 14 days.
* You have receipts that still need reviewing.
* Your contractor added a new job.
* A payment has been issued.
* Your tax savings increased by $42 after your latest expense.

This becomes the first card on the dashboard and doubles as an in-app notification center. It makes the portal feel like it’s actively helping the subcontractor rather than just displaying data.

If you keep building in this direction—action-oriented, simple, and tax-aware—I think your subcontractor portal will stand out from generic contractor software because it’s actually designed around how independent subcontractors work day to day.
