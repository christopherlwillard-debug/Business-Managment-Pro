One final thought

I think your app is starting to mature enough that it needs to stop thinking in terms of one giant Job Card.

Instead, think of it as:

Production Workspace
Service Workspace
General Contracting Workspace

All three still create jobs in the same database, use the same financials, photos, invoices, subcontractors, and scheduling.

The difference is each workspace only shows the tools that are relevant to that type of work.

That keeps the interface much cleaner without changing your underlying data model.

I think that's the direction that will make the app feel much more professional. It isn't removing functionality—it's showing the right functionality at the right time. When you create a Manufactured Home Production job, you shouldn't even see estimating features because you'll never use them there. When you create a General Contracting job, those same tools become front and center because that's where they add value. That's a much more natural workflow than trying to fit every feature into one form.
