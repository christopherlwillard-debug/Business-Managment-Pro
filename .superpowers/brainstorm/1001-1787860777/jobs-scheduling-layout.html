B — Card-first with collapsed summary

The top strip stays one line until opened. Each active Job Card carries its own “Next” item. Best for minimal scrolling and phone use.

Shared behavior

  • Only active/open jobs participate
  • Skipped, inactive-section, and completed items are excluded
  • Only the next 1–2 scheduled items appear per card
  • Full checklist remains behind the existing Checklist action

Recommendation

  • Choose B for the shortest page and strongest mobile experience
  • Choose A if the week-at-a-glance strip must always be open
