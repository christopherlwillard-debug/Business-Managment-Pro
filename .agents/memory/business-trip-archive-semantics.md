---
name: Business trip archive semantics
description: Historical totals and active audit-readiness use different trip scopes.
---

Archived business trips remain part of historical trip counts, travel spend, receipt counts, and deductible meal totals. Archived trips are excluded from audit-attention lists and active-trip detail tables.

**Why:** Archiving preserves real historical travel records without making closed trips block current documentation readiness.

**How to apply:** Label totals as historical when archived trips are included, and scope readiness warnings and active audit lists to non-archived trips.
