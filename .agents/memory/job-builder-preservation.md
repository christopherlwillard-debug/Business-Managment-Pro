---
name: Job Builder preservation rule
description: Durable scope boundary for redesigning the BuildPro Job Builder and job workspace.
---

For Job Builder and job workspace experience changes, preserve existing fields, records, calculations, and workflows; reorganize the experience rather than removing capabilities.

**Why:** the owner explicitly requires continuity with the current application.

**How to apply:** keep business-specific paths, authoritative ledgers and calculations, and existing save, completion, closeout, and reopening safeguards; audit the current paths before proposing UI changes.
