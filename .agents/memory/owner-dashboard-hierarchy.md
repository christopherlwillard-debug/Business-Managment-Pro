**Rule:** Prioritize Needs Attention, then Today/Next, then the business snapshot, with secondary information afterward. Keep the receipt scan as a compact quick action. Show loading, empty, and failed states distinctly; never present unavailable or unknown financial data as zero.

**Why:** The owner reviewed the signed-in Dashboard after the redesign and said the layout looked right.

**How to apply:** For future owner Dashboard changes, preserve this hierarchy and the approved business-scope rules in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-dashboard-business-scope-design.md`. Keep existing financial calculations and source ownership; company-level compliance/tax stays company-level and cash flow stays All Businesses-only.
